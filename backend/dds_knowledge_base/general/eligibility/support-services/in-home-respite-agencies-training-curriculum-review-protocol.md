# In-Home Respite Agencies - Training Curriculum Review Protocol : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/eligibility/support-services/in-home-respite-agencies-training-curriculum-review-protocol/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/eligibility/support-services/in-home-respite-agencies-training-curriculum-review-protocol/)

---

The training curriculum review protocol is provided to assist In-Home Respite Agencies in developing training to respite workers in the provision of incidental medical services – gastrostomy feeding, colostomy/ileostomy, and urinary catheter care. Incidental medical services were added to the Lanterman Developmental Disabilities Services Act (Welfare and Institutions Code [Section 4686](<http://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes.xhtml#4686>)) by the 2009 Statutes, Assembly Bill 4th Extraordinary, Chapter 9. The Department of Developmental Services (DDS) developed the review protocol with input from regional center clinicians.

## Checklist

The three review protocol checklists below outline specific details of how DDS will review each proposed training curriculum for respective procedures.

  * [Gastrostomy (G-Tube) Checklist](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/IMS_GastrostomyTrainingProtocol_Checklist_June2021.pdf>)
  * [Colostomy and Ileostomy Checklist](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/ColostomyIleostomyTrainingCirriculm_Checklist_June2021.pdf>)
  * [Urinary Catheter Checklist](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/UrinaryCathCurriculum_Checklist_June2021.pdf>)

The training curriculum for each procedure the respite agency plans to provide must be submitted to DDS for final approval. Please submit the training curriculum via email to [ClinicalServices@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:ClinicalServices@dds.ca.gov>) or mail to:

Department of Developmental Services
