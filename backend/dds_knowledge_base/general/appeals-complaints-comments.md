## Overview of the Complaints and Appeals Processes

The state’s developmental services community is best served by an open exchange of information and opinions, and DDS is committed to resolving problems and concerns when they occur. Below are the multiple options for individuals served by regional center, family members, vendors and providers have for filing appeals and complaints.

If you are an individual receiving regional center services and want to file a complaint against a regional center, DDS or vendor, which may include but not limited to allegations of discrimination, retaliation and/or bias, please send your complaint to [Appeals@dds.ca.gov](<>). For more information on the Civil Rights Department and how to submit a complaint to the California Department of Civil Rights the website is: [CRD | Civil Rights Department](<https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/>) and/or Federally at: [Civil Rights Division | Civil Rights Division](<https://www.justice.gov/crt>).

## [Appeals Process – for individuals age 3 or over](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/fair-hearings-complaint-process/>)

Used to resolve disputes with a regional center or state-operated facility about eligibility for services or the nature, scope, or amount of services and supports a person should be receiving.

_*Not to be used for Individual rights violations for an individual served – For rights violations of an individual served see_[ _Individual Rights Complaint Process_](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/consumer-rights-complaint/>) _._

[View Flow Chart](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/OCAR_AppealsFlowChart_English_20250508.pdf>)

## [Individual Rights Complaint Process](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/consumer-rights-complaint/>)

Used when an individual served by regional center, or any representative acting on behalf of an individual served by regional center, believes a right (or rights) have been unfairly denied by a regional center, state-operated facility, or a service provider.

_*Not to be used for disputes about eligibility for services or the nature, scope or type of services an individual is receiving – For eligibility or service appeals see_[ _Appeals Process_](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/fair-hearings-complaint-process/>) _._

[View Flow Chart](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/OCAR_4731FlowChart_20250508.pdf>)

## [Title 17 Complaint Procedures](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/title-17-complaint-procedure/>)

This procedure is to be used when: (1) one of the “personal rights” of an individual who resides in a state-operated facility, community care or health care facility, has been denied; and (2) the individual receiving services from the regional center disagrees.

## Early Start Complaint Processes – for children aged 0-3 yrs

[Early Start Information Packet](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/new-information-packet/>)

Early Start services are for children aged 0-3 who are at risk of having a developmental disability or have a developmental disability or delay, and their families.

There are three separate processes in place for addressing disagreements under Early Start.

  * [**Early Start Mediation Conference Request**](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/early-start-mediation-conference-requests/>) – Voluntary mediation to informally resolve disagreements between parents and a regional center and/or local education agency (LEA) related to alleged violation(s) of federal or state laws governing California’s Early Start Program.

*Mediation can be requested as the first option for resolution, or during a complaint or due process hearing process, if the parent believes mediation might be more appropriate.

  * [**Early Start Due Process Hearing**](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/early-start-due-process-hearing-requests/>) – the Early Start Due Process procedure is a more formal process to resolve disagreements between families and a regional center and/or LEA related to a proposal or refusal for identification, evaluation, assessment, placement, or services.

*Due process hearing decisions are final unless appealed to the superior court of appropriate jurisdiction or civil action is brought by any aggrieved parties.

  * [**The Early Start State Complaint Process**](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/early-start-complaint-process/>) – the purpose of the State complaint process is to investigate and resolve any alleged violations of federal or state laws or regulations governing California’s Early Start program including eligibility and services. Parents or any individual, agency or organization may file an Early Start State Complaint.

## [Vendorization Appeals](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/vendorization-appeals/>)

Used to appeal a denial of vendorization application, termination of vendorization, or failure of a regional center to comply with regulations.

## [Residential Facility Appeals](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/residential-facility-appeals/>)

Where a vendored community care facility may appeal the actions taken by a regional center regarding service level disapproval, sanctions, findings of substantial inadequacy or immediate danger, or enforcement of any requirement by the regional center which is not contained in Title 17, California Code of Regulations.

## [Rate Appeals](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/rate-appeals/>)

Used for a vendor to appeal a rate set by the Department of Developmental Services, the effective date of the rate, or the denial of a rate adjustment.

## Family Home Agency Appeals

This process can be used by a FHA applicant or vendor to appeal the following regional center actions: denial or termination of FHA vendorization; movement of a consumer from a family home approved by a vendored FHA; and/or revocation of a family home’s Certificate of Approval. The appeal process is defined in Title 17 of the California Code of Regulations Section [56096](<https://govt.westlaw.com/calregs/Document/I75F92170D60711DE88AEDDE29ED1DC0A?originationContext=Search+Result&listSource=Search&viewType=FullText&navigationPath=Search%2fv3%2fsearch%2fresults%2fnavigation%2fi0ad7140a0000016abd74b5c2857825d7%3fstartIndex%3d1%26Nav%3dREGULATION_PUBLICVIEW%26contextData%3d\(sc.Default\)&rank=1&list=REGULATION_PUBLICVIEW&transitionType=SearchItem&contextData=\(sc.Search\)&t_T1=17&t_T2=56096&t_S1=CA+ADC+s>) and [56097](<https://govt.westlaw.com/calregs/Document/I7631BDA0D60711DE88AEDDE29ED1DC0A?originationContext=Search+Result&listSource=Search&viewType=FullText&navigationPath=Search%2fv3%2fsearch%2fresults%2fnavigation%2fi0ad7140b0000016abd74ecadb2d842a0%3fstartIndex%3d1%26Nav%3dREGULATION_PUBLICVIEW%26contextData%3d\(sc.Default\)&rank=1&list=REGULATION_PUBLICVIEW&transitionType=SearchItem&contextData=\(sc.Search\)&t_T1=17&t_T2=56097&t_S1=CA+ADC+s>). The FHA must appeal in writing to the DDS Director within 30 days after receiving notice from the regional center of intended action.

## Vendor Fiscal Audit Appeals

Vendors wishing to appeal a fiscal audit report issued by a regional center or DDS may do so by submitting a written request to DDS within 30 days after receipt of the report or findings of fact. Requests may be mailed to:

Office of Legal Affairs

Department of Developmental Services

P.O. Box 944202

Sacramento, CA 94299-9974
