# Language Access Complaint Process : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/language-access-complaint-process/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/appeals-complaints-comments/language-access-complaint-process/)

---

The Department of Developmental Services (DDS) is committed to providing the public equal access to information about its programs, services, and activities. This includes making information available in non-English languages for persons who are non-English speaking or who have limited English proficiency, in accordance with applicable state and federal laws and executive orders.

If you feel DDS was unable to serve you because of a language barrier (limited-English proficiency) or other communication differences, you may a file a language access complaint using DDS’ Language Access Complaint Form (DS 6022).

  * [DS 6022 – English](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/DS_6022_English.pdf>)
  * [DS 6022 – Spanish](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/DS_6022_Spanish.pdf>)
  * [DS 6022 – Chinese (Simplified)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/DS_6022_Chinese-Simplified.pdf>)
  * [DS 6022 – Chinese (Traditional)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/DS_6022_Chinese-Traditional.pdf>)
  * [DS 6022 – Tagalog](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/DS_6022_Tagalog.pdf>)
  * [DS 6022 – Vietnamese](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/DS_6022_Vietnamese.pdf>)
  * [DS 6022 – Korean](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/DS_6022_Korean.pdf>)

DDS evaluates all language access complaints on a case-by-case basis to determine if additional communication assistance may be indicated as well as identify opportunities to foster effective and meaningful departmentwide communication practices.

A representative from DDS may contact you to discuss your language access complaint, collect additional information, and confirm the information or services you need.
