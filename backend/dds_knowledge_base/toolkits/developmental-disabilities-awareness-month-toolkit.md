# Developmental Disabilities Awareness Month Toolkit : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/toolkits/developmental-disabilities-awareness-month-toolkit/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/toolkits/developmental-disabilities-awareness-month-toolkit/)

---

This Developmental Disabilities Awareness Month, the Department’s **Everyone Belongs** social media campaign aims to highlight the importance of inclusion and everyone’s right to make choices, build connections, and participate fully in their community.

The Department created the image gallery below for our state and community partners. They were designed to be shared with your audiences across your channels. When sharing, you can tag the Department at @caldds_ on Instagram, @CaliforniaDDS on Facebook and X, and DDSCareers on LinkedIn. Please email [pio@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:pio@dds.ca.gov>) with questions.

**Sample Post Text**

This March, we recognize Developmental Disabilities Awareness Month by celebrating inclusion, access, and community. Everyone deserves to be welcomed, valued, and seen—and to have what they need to participate fully in everyday life.

People with developmental disabilities are neighbors, coworkers, friends, and community members. When barriers are removed and opportunities are accessible, everyone benefits.

Join California’s Department of Developmental Services and regional centers in honoring the experiences, contributions, and rights of people with developmental disabilities—this month and every month.

#Everyone Belongs #CaliforniaIDD #DDAM2026
