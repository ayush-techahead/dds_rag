# Free Support for Your Family with BrightLife Kids : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/free-support-for-your-family-with-brightlife-kids/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/free-support-for-your-family-with-brightlife-kids/)

> The holiday season is often a whirlwind, bringing with it stress and big feelings for kids and caregivers alike. BrightLife Kids shares some tips on how to stress less and make more space for joy during the holidays.

---

The holiday season is often a whirlwind, bringing with it stress and big feelings for kids and caregivers alike. We want to share a valuable, free resource for your families: [BrightLife Kids](<https://www.brightlife.kids/>).

This California-funded program is dedicated to giving children (ages 0-12) and their families the tools they need to build resilience, manage stress, and thrive. No insurance or referrals are required to access these supports, which include:

  * Live 1:1 coaching support and secure chat 

  * Personalized digital tools

  * Peer communities for caregivers

  * Referrals to local community resources

We also invite you to read their latest articles on mental health and the holidays:

  * [Holiday happiness: How to model balance and joy for your kids](<https://www.hellobrightline.com/learning-hub/blog/finding-holiday-happiness/>)
