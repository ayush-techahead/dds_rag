# Get connected to California’s Autism Services! : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/get-connected-to-californias-autism-services/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/get-connected-to-californias-autism-services/)

> The Autism Services Branch coordinates and oversees services for people with autism in California.

---

### Get to know the Autism Services Branch

In response to the growing number of individuals diagnosed with autism spectrum disorder, the Autism Services Branch was established on July 1, 2023, to coordinate and oversee services for people with autism in California. The Branch aims to get more individuals and their families connected to supports and services to live the life they choose. This includes Early Start services, various therapies, employment supports, and more. In celebration of Autism Month, we asked the team questions about their work and what they want you to know about autism.

### Reach out to the Autism Helpline

If you have questions about autism or the services and supports available, the DDS Autism Helpline is a great place to start. Please note the Helpline is not a 24/7 crisis line. Call 833-815-2337 or email [autism@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:autism@dds.ca.gov>).

### Check out the Autism Hub

Visit the DDS Autism Resource Hub at [www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/autism](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/autism/>) for information and tools related to autism for self-advocates, families, caregivers, direct support professionals, providers, and other partners.
