# Be Wildfire Ready : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/be-wildfire-ready/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/be-wildfire-ready/)

---

It’s officially Fall, and wildfire season continues! Are you prepared to respond if a wildfire is nearby? In California, wildfires typically begin in late Spring and can continue into late November. Fire experts say lush green landscapes caused by a winter of heavy rain can turn into dangerous fuel for wildfires in high-risk areas. Be ready! Prepare your property and make a plan with your families, friends, and neighbors.
