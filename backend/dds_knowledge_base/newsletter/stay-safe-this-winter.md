The winter season is officially underway, and we’d like you to make it a happy one!

Our latest Emergency Preparedness Bulletin covers topics like:

  * How to be ready for power outages

  * What to do if you’re stuck in your car

  * How to prepare for storms and high tides

  * How to avoid falling prey to text, voicemail and email scams

  * Links to other emergency resources.
