# New Year, New Job? : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-year-new-job/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-year-new-job/)

---

Are you thinking about a career change or a fresh start this year? The Department is hiring!

We’re looking for people with a passion for public service who are eager to make a real difference in people’s lives.

If that sounds like you, explore our current available positions at [www.dds.ca.gov/careers](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/careers/>). We offer meaningful work, competitive pay, great benefits, and a defined-benefit pension plan.
