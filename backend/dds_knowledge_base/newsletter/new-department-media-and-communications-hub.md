# New Department Media and Communications Hub : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-department-media-and-communications-hub/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-department-media-and-communications-hub/)

---

The Office of Communications has a new webpage to keep you informed and make it easier for you to help spread the word! Explore the New [Media and Communications Hub](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/media-and-communications-hub/>) to get:

  * **News.** The latest press releases, newsletter entries, and updates from the Department all in one place.
  * **Digital toolkits**. Ready-to-use social media content and images, blurbs for websites and newsletters, and more for various initiatives and services.
  * **Brand Resources.** Guidelines and forms to support your outreach in partnership with the Department.

Whether you’re looking for the latest announcements or storytelling that brings our mission to life, the Media and Communications hub provides tools to help get the word out about services and supports available to Californians with intellectual and developmental disabilities and their families. Please send any questions or feedback to [pio@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:pio@dds.ca.gov>).
