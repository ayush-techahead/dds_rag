# See How Senate Bill 138 Initiatives are Progressing : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/how-senate-bill-138-initiatives-are-progressing/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/how-senate-bill-138-initiatives-are-progressing/)

> A new document outlines how statewide standardization efforts required under Senate Bill 138 are moving forward. The update covers timelines and progress across key initiatives impacting services and data practices.

---

New and changed laws in 2023 required statewide standardization of some processes and assessments, and improvements to data collection. This [Senate Bill 138 Initiatives summary](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/SB138_Summary_20251202.pdf>) document describes these requirements and provides an update on the work being done to meet the requirements. The timeline picture below shows the status of each initiative, including:

  * Generic Services and Common Regional Center Services

  * New and Updated Data Definitions

  * Respite Tool Template and Processes

  * Intake Processes

  * Vendorization Procedures

  * Individual Program Plan Template and Procedures
