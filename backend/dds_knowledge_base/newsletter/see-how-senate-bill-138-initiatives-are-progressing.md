# See How Senate Bill 138 Initiatives are Progressing : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/see-how-senate-bill-138-initiatives-are-progressing/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/see-how-senate-bill-138-initiatives-are-progressing/)

---

New and changing laws in 2023 required statewide standardization of some processes and assessments, and improvements to data collection. This [Senate Bill 138 Initiatives summary](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/SB-138-Initiatives-Summary-January-2025.pdf>) document describes the [law’s](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240SB138>) requirements and provides an update on the work being done to meet the requirements. The timeline picture below shows the status of each initiative, including:

  * Generic Services and Common Regional Center Services
  * New and Updated Data Definitions
  * Respite Assessment Template and Processes
  * Intake Processes
  * Vendorization Procedures
  * Individual Program Plan (IPP) Template and Procedures
