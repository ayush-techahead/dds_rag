# Join the Consumer Advisory Committee : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/join-the-consumer-advisory-committee/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/join-the-consumer-advisory-committee/)

---

The Department’s Consumer Advisory Committee (CAC) provides advice on policies, programs, legislation, and regulations that affect the lives of Californians with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

  * Fifteen (15) self-advocate members from all over California
  * Meets at least four (4) times per year in Sacramento, or online via Zoom.
  * Members share information with their local self-advocacy organizations and bring input from those groups back to the CAC.

If you would like to apply to be a CAC member, here is what is needed:

  * You must have an intellectual or developmental disability
  * You must be receiving regional center services.
  * You must be a member of a local self-advocacy group or other organization that serves or empowers people with developmental disabilities.
  * That organization must nominate you for the committee.
  * Fill out the online [CAC application](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/consumers/consumer-advisory-committee/>) and submit it with your nomination letter.
