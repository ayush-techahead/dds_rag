Love helping people? Being part of a great team with an amazing purpose? We are hiring! Good salaries, great benefits, fun co-workers. See all of our job descriptions here: [https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/careers/](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/careers/>). Take an exam, apply for the position you are interested in, and prepare for an interview! Also, [www.jobs.ca.gov](<http://www.jobs.ca.gov/>) has more information and tips to help you learn about and get through the State’s civil service system. We look forward to talking with you about your future!
