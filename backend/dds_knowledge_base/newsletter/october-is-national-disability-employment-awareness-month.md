# October is National Disability Employment Awareness Month! : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/october-is-national-disability-employment-awareness-month/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/october-is-national-disability-employment-awareness-month/)

---

California joins the nation in celebrating the many ways people with disabilities are making the workforce better! Governor Gavin Newsom proclaimed October as [National Disability Employment Awareness Month.](<https://www.gov.ca.gov/2024/10/03/governor-newsom-proclaims-disability-employment-awareness-month-2024/>) In recognition, the Department is highlighting how Californians are working together to grow meaningful job opportunities. We’re also sharing stories from Californians with intellectual and developmental disabilities who thrive in jobs they enjoy. Their stories highlight that every person can succeed and contribute their talents.
