# Family Fee Programs Ended : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/family-fee-programs-ended/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/family-fee-programs-ended/)

> The Family Cost Participation Program and Annual Family Program Fee were repealed effective July 1, 2024, and the Parental Fee Program was repealed effective July 1, 2025.

---

The Family Cost Participation Program and Annual Family Program Fee were repealed effective July 1, 2024, and the Parental Fee Program was repealed effective July 1, 2025.

These fees were collected from individuals and families to help fund certain programs. It was expensive to calculate each family’s fees and for individuals and families to pay them. In the end, the money collected was not enough to meaningfully improve services for people.

The Department will finish collection activities for Parental Fee Program fees assessed through June 30, 2025. New fees will not be assessed after that date.
