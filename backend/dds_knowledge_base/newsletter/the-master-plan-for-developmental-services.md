# The Master Plan for Developmental Services : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/the-master-plan-for-developmental-services/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/the-master-plan-for-developmental-services/)

---

The [Master Plan](<https://www.chhs.ca.gov/home/master-plan-for-developmental-services/>) for Developmental Services seeks to create and strengthen bridges that connect the developmental services system to other critical systems across CalHHS and beyond. This includes agencies that support education, housing, employment, transportation, and safety. Each meeting is open to the public and includes participation of community members statewide.
