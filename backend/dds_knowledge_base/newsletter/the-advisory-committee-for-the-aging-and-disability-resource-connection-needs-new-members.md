# The Advisory Committee for the Aging and Disability Resource Connection needs new members : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/the-advisory-committee-for-the-aging-and-disability-resource-connection-needs-new-members/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/the-advisory-committee-for-the-aging-and-disability-resource-connection-needs-new-members/)

> The Advisory Committee for the Aging and Disability Resource Connection (ADRC) Program is looking for new members! 

---

The [Advisory Committee](<https://urldefense.com/v3/__https:/aging.ca.gov/Providers_and_Partners/Aging_and_Disability_Resource_Connection/Advisory_Committee/__;!!Jv0_iA8!v2EVOZAqojoFE-t22nw2sRDNmhcZyQd1BLP3aZIGkr3g-el_aydha0uIXajo8ZYZqiHRB7HNzMtm8lKztRmV5TZEXiZDm9_s$>) for the Aging and Disability Resource Connection (ADRC) Program at the California Department of Aging is looking for new members! The ADRC Advisory Committee advises the Department of Aging on long-term services and supports, such as paid and unpaid medical and personal care services that assist with activities of daily living (eating, bathing, and dressing) and instrumental activities of daily living (preparing meals, managing medication, and housekeeping).

The Advisory Committee wants people with a broad and diverse representation that includes:

  * Experts in long-term services and supports
  * People with lived experience receiving these services
  * People caring for someone receiving these services

There are 10 open seats on the 19-member committee. For more information, please review the [Announcement Letter](<https://urldefense.com/v3/__https:/aging.ca.gov/download.ashx?lE0rcNUV0zbBxOatw*2fMTVw*3d*3d__;JSUl!!Jv0_iA8!v2EVOZAqojoFE-t22nw2sRDNmhcZyQd1BLP3aZIGkr3g-el_aydha0uIXajo8ZYZqiHRB7HNzMtm8lKztRmV5TZEXtjN69GT$>).

  * [Membership Applications](<https://urldefense.com/v3/__https:/aging.ca.gov/download.ashx?lE0rcNUV0zZfqAivmw6bRA*3d*3d__;JSU!!Jv0_iA8!v2EVOZAqojoFE-t22nw2sRDNmhcZyQd1BLP3aZIGkr3g-el_aydha0uIXajo8ZYZqiHRB7HNzMtm8lKztRmV5TZEXt3l8xJ4$>) can be submitted via email to [ADRC@aging.ca.gov](<mailto:ADRC@aging.ca.gov>)
  * Applications received by April 30, 2025, will be considered for a two-year membership beginning July 2025
