# Harbor Regional Center’s Annual Backpack Giveaway was a huge hit! : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/harbor-regional-centers-annual-backpack-giveaway-was-a-huge-hit/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/harbor-regional-centers-annual-backpack-giveaway-was-a-huge-hit/)

[Harbor Regional Center](<https://www.harborrc.org/>) (Harbor) staff packed and distributed approximately 200 backpacks at their Annual Backpack Giveaway. Over 80 individuals and families stopped by Harbor’s Torrance office on August 12, getting ready for an exciting new school year. Another 200 backpacks are on the way to students who could not make the event.

### Supporting Students Year-Round

Harbor supports people with intellectual and developmental disabilities every step of the way. This includes the all-important school years, from age 3 to 22! Your Service Coordinator can help you navigate the educational system. They can:

  * Help parents and caregivers prepare for Individualized Education Program (IEP) meetings

  * Discuss your child’s needs, goals, and concerns

  * Join you at the IEP meeting, if requested

  * Connect you with an Education Specialist or share advocacy resources to make sure you have the tools you need.

For students in transition programs (ages 18–22), service coordinators also can help explore work experience programs. These programs help students gain valuable job skills and confidence in real-world work settings.
