# Stay Safe This Summer: Get Our Safety Bulletin : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/stay-safe-this-summer-get-our-safety-bulletin/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/stay-safe-this-summer-get-our-safety-bulletin/)

---

June 20th marked the official start of Summer 2025!

We’ve created a quick and easy-to-read Summer Heat Safety Bulletin filled with tips, resources, and reminders to help you enjoy the season and stay safe. [Download or print this year’s summer bulletin](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/EmergencySummerEdition6_17_25.pdf>). Translations will soon be made available on the [Emergency Preparedness page on our website](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/consumers/emergency-preparedness-consumer>).

You can also download our Excessive Heat bulletin linked below. Parts of California have already experienced several extreme heat days. As the summer season heats up, the risk for heat-related illness or accidents increases. Take steps now to prepare and care for yourself, the people you support, and your loved ones.
