**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/get-started-explore-the-guide-to-californias-regional-center-system-for-individuals-and-families/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/get-started-explore-the-guide-to-californias-regional-center-system-for-individuals-and-families/)

> Learn all about developmental services in California with a new, easy-to-use information packet on the California Department of Developmental Services website!

---

Learn all about developmental services in California with a new, easy-to-use information packet! With input from our community, the Department has developed the [Guide to California’s Regional Center System](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/consumers/new-information-packet/>). It was designed for anyone interested in learning more about regional center services. Inside, you’ll find information about:

  * What regional centers do

  * Your rights as an individual who receives services or family member

  * Services and supports available

  * How to find out if you are eligible for services

Download your own copy of the Guide to California’s Regional Center System or the Early Start Standardized Information Packet on our webpage [hyperlink regional center webpage with packets]. The information packets will be available in multiple languages to make them accessible to all. Audio and American Sign Language versions are being developed, too!
