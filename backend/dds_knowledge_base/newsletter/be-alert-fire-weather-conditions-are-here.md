Hot, dry, and windy conditions make wildfires more likely to happen and spread fast, according to our partners at [CalFire](<https://www.fire.ca.gov/>). Review the following tips to help prevent wildfires in your community!

**Stay Smart, Safe, and Spark-Free**

  *     * Never park or drive over dry grass

  * Avoid using fire outdoors, like grilling or campfires, if the fire could spread to dry plants nearby

  * Secure trailer chains to avoid sparks on the road

  * Skip using spark-producing equipment

For more wildfire prevention tips, visit: [ReadyforWildfire.org](<https://readyforwildfire.org/>).

Sign up for accurate updates about wildfire near you with [Fire Planner](<https://plan.readyforwildfire.org/en/incidents>).

Important Reminder! Be sure to keep your contact information current with your [regional center](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/listings/>) service coordinator so they can reach you during an emergency.
