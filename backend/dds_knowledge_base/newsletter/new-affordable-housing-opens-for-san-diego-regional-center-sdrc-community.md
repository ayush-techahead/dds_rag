# New Affordable Housing Opens for San Diego Regional Center (SDRC) Community : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-affordable-housing-opens-for-san-diego-regional-center-sdrc-community/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-affordable-housing-opens-for-san-diego-regional-center-sdrc-community/)

> Three new housing developments in San Diego are expanding access to inclusive, long-term homes for individuals and families served by SDRC. These communities will offer services and stability for people across the region.

---

California continues to expand inclusive, affordable housing for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities and their families.

On October 14, 2025, community partners gathered in San Diego to celebrate the grand opening of the Modica Family Apartments and Taormina Family Apartments in Clairemont. Led by Chelsea Investments Corporations in partnership with the San Diego Regional Center (SDRC), both developments have modern features such as a community room, computer resource center, playgrounds, and underground parking.

Between the two developments, 58 apartments are set aside for individuals and families supported by SDRC.

![Eleven people in suits stand behind a red ribbon in front of a building.](https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/harrington-Image-67-250x312.jpg)

The grand opening of the Harrington Heights.

Just days earlier, on October 8, another affordable housing development called Harrington Heights celebrated its grand opening. Harrington Heights includes 40 units set aside for individuals and families served by SDRC.

These three developments support efforts across San Diego and throughout California to expand access to long-term safe, inclusive housing in the community for individuals receiving regional center services.
