# Learn about Communication Service Options: FAQs Now Available in 11 Languages : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/learn-about-communication-service-options-faqs-now-available-in-11-languages/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/learn-about-communication-service-options-faqs-now-available-in-11-languages/)

> You and your family can learn about options and your right to communicate in all parts of life with the Communications Services FAQ.

---

For some people, communication works differently. Some of us may use support in the form of interventions, training, and technologies to communicate more effectively. Figuring out the right support can be complex and depends on each person’s unique communication needs.

A Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) document for [Communication Services](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/CommunicationServicesFAQ_English_20250708.pdf>) explains:

  * What communication services are and how they can help

  * Who can benefit from them

  * Different ways people communicate, such as American Sign Language (ASL), communication boards, or speech-generating devices

  * How families can access communication supports through schools, regional centers, or health providers

Available in 11 languages, the Communication Services FAQ document can help you and your family learn about options and your right to communicate in all parts of life.
