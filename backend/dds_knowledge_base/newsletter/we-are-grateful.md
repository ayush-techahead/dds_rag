# We Are Grateful : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/we-are-grateful/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/we-are-grateful/)

> Thank you, Bridges readers, and all of you who have shared your stories with us this past year. As we plan for the months ahead, we want to hear from you!

---

We want to take a moment to thank you, our readers.

Thank you for being part of this work and for supporting the stories and information we share each month. Your engagement and generosity help us connect more people to the information they need. You lift the voices of Californians with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

We’re also especially grateful to everyone who allowed us to share pieces of their lives this year, whether it was an accomplishment, a challenge they overcame, or a perspective they wanted others to hear.

## Help us plan for the months ahead!

We’d love to hear from you about what you’d like to see in this newsletter. Your input can help us choose stories and updates that support and reflect our community.

What would make you look forward to each issue of this Bridges newsletter? Email your ideas and feedback to [PIO@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:PIO@dds.ca.gov>).
