# Looking for a Job? The DSP Internship Let’s You Earn While You Learn! : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/looking-for-a-job-the-dsp-internship-lets-you-earn-while-you-learn/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/looking-for-a-job-the-dsp-internship-lets-you-earn-while-you-learn/)

> No experience? No problem! The Direct Support Professional (DSP) Internship Program pays you while you train to support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities. Start your journey to becoming a DSP today!

---

Are you ready to find a job where you can earn money and gain valuable skills at the same time? The Direct Support Professional (DSP) Internship Program is a great opportunity to get hands-on-experience supporting people with intellectual and developmental disabilities! 

No experience? No problem! This paid training program will teach you everything need to know to start a rewarding career. You will train alongside a trained and experienced DSP.

Interested in becoming a DSP intern? Or are you a provider looking to mentor the next generation of DSPs? Connect with the DDS Workforce Team! Visit the [DSP Internship Program website](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/workforce-initiatives-4/dsp-internship-program/>).
