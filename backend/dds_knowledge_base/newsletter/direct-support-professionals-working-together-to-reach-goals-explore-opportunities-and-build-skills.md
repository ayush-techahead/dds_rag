# Direct Support Professionals: Working Together to Reach Goals, Explore Opportunities, and Build Skills : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/direct-support-professionals-working-together-to-reach-goals-explore-opportunities-and-build-skills/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/direct-support-professionals-working-together-to-reach-goals-explore-opportunities-and-build-skills/)

> From September 7-13, we shared stories on social media of DSPs across the State who have been recognized by their organization for excellence. See how five amazing DSPs are making a difference.

---

Please join us in thanking all of the direct support professionals (DSPs) who work with Californians with intellectual and developmental disabilities to reach their goals at home, at work, and in our communities!

DSPs are celebrated nationally each September during DSP Recognition Week. This year, the Department and California Disability Services Association (CDSA) partnered to highlight the important work DSPs do each day. From September 7-13, we shared stories on social media of DSPs across the State who have been recognized by their organization for excellence.

Here are stories about five amazing DSPs you can see on our [Instagram](<https://www.instagram.com/caldds_>) and [Facebook](<https://www.facebook.com/CaliforniaDDS/>) accounts:

**![Gary Lewis](https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/garyLewis-150x150.jpg)Gary Lewis**  
_Direct Support Professional  
Arc of San Diego  
San Diego, Calif._  
Gary doesn’t just support people with their jobs — he’s there for each person as they grow in every part of life. Gary teaches workplace skills to build confidence and communication.

**![Elin Herval showing a book to a lady who's sitting](https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/elinHervall-150x150.jpg)Elin Hervall**  
_Direct Support Professional  
Marin Ventures  
San Rafael, Calif._  
Elin finds new doors to open to new opportunities for the people she supports. She leads activities such as reading, kayaking, swimming, or exploring the community. She is known for being kind and creative. Elin is always making sure participants have a voice and the chance to thrive.

![](https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Vanny-with-Client-1-150x150.jpg)

**Vanny Seang**  
_Direct Support Professional_  
_Lifehouse Agency, Inc.  
San Rafael, Calif._  
Vanny has shown extraordinary commitment to support each person to succeed. She brings joy and stability to every person she supports.

![Jacob Estillore](https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/jacobestillore-150x150.jpg)**Jacob Estillore**  
_Direct Support Professional  
AbilityPath  
Burlingame, Calif._  
Jacob leads a baking program that teaches teamwork and decision-making. Participants light up in his presence. Colleagues say his positivity strengthens the entire team.

**![Laura Zore](https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/lauraZore-150x150.jpg)Laura Zore**  
_Community Support Facilitator Lead  
Hope Services  
Gilroy, Calif._  
Laura is a trusted, fun, and constant supportive presence for everyone she’s around. Her colleagues and people she supports say she’s always willing to jump in to overcome any challenge. Laura finds opportunities for each person to achieve their life goals.
