# January 2025 Wildfire Response and Recovery : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/january-2025-wildfire-response-and-recovery/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/january-2025-wildfire-response-and-recovery/)

---

Community members and partners, 

As the wildfires continue to affect our southern communities, we send our deepest gratitude to the firefighters, first responders, regional center employees, and community members protecting and supporting everyone impacted by the southern and central California wildfires. Our hearts remain with the individuals and families facing disruptions and unimaginable loss.

The safety and well-being of individuals and families remain the top priority. Please be aware of and use the following resources and support available in your area during these challenging times. Please also remember you are not alone in this. If you or a loved one needs assistance, you can contact your service coordinator or regional center. They are ready to provide guidance, connect you with local resources, and help navigate the recovery process.

### **Resources for Response and Recovery**

Support is available to address immediate needs, such as temporary housing, medical care, and emotional health:

  * The Governor’s office has compiled a list of critical resources at [www.ca.gov/LAfires](<http://www.ca.gov/LAfires>). 
  * All Los Angeles County [regional centers](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/lookup-rcs-by-county/>) have response information on their websites.
  * CalHHS has an [Emergency Guide](<https://www.chhs.ca.gov/blog/2025/01/08/calhhs-emergency-resource-guide/?fbclid=IwY2xjawHuZQtleHRuA2FlbQIxMAABHTqq1ANlUc1ZajImF0V__ifFuf8jM2yc2cRe5jtrA2JDmW6qFNu9lOmg3A_aem_WUP4EbefG6sxJTUJeZzSDg>) that centralizes resources for mental health, social services, recovery, and more. 
  * [Fire.ca.gov](<https://www.fire.ca.gov/>) and [Listos.ca.gov](<https://www.listoscalifornia.org/disaster-readiness/wildfires/>) provide updates and resources during active and upcoming emergency events.
  * The Department’s emergency preparedness team has gathered [local resources](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/consumers/emergency-preparedness-consumer/>) and guides for immigrants in [English](<https://www.cdss.ca.gov/Portals/13/DisasterAssistanceGuideforImmigrantCaliforniansFinal.pdf>) and [Spanish.](<https://www.cdss.ca.gov/Portals/13/DisasterAssistanceGuideforImmigrantCaliforniansSpanishFinal.pdf>)
  * [Guide to Disaster Assistance Services for Immigrant Californians](<https://www.cdss.ca.gov/Portals/13/DisasterAssistanceGuideforImmigrantCaliforniansFinal.pdf>)[ – Spanish](<https://www.cdss.ca.gov/Portals/13/DisasterAssistanceGuideforImmigrantCaliforniansSpanishFinal.pdf>)
  * CalOES Public Service Announcement (January 13, 2025) [Southern California Wildfire-Themed Scams](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Cal-CSIC_PSA_WildfireThemedScams.pdf>)
  * [CalOES Shelter Information](<https://news.caloes.ca.gov/shelters-available-for-communities-impacted-by-wildfires-in-los-angeles-county-2/>) Emergency shelter listing.

### **Steps to Take After a Wildfire**

The [Governor’s Office](<https://www.ca.gov/LAfires/>) and CalFire has recommendations for steps you can take in the [aftermath of a wildfire](<https://readyforwildfire.org/post-wildfire/>), including:

  * A checklist for returning home safely
  * Who can help
  * Tips and tools for rebuilding
  * Additional resources for long-term recovery

### **How you can help**

We all can support our southern California communities and neighbors by: 

  * Donating to regional center relief funds for individuals and families:
    * San Gabriel/Pamona Regional Center [Mail-In Donations](<https://conta.cc/40sYOL7>)
    * Westside Regional Center [Fire Relief Fund](<https://westsiderc.org/donate-2/>)
    * Frank D. Lanterman Regional Center [Fire Relief Fund](<https://lanterman.org/donate/>)
    * Harbor Regional Center [Help Fund](<https://www.harborrc.org/get-involved/support-the-harbor-help-fund/>)
    * North Los Angeles County Regional Center [FRNC Fund](<https://www.arcanet.org/your-regional-center/your-regional-center-rc-help-funds/>)
  * [Donate items from the Amazon Wishlist](<https://nacdd.org/la-fires-devastating-impact-on-disability-community/>) for impacted I/DD families; managed by the National Association of Councils on Developmental Disabilities and The Colin Farrell Foundation.
  * Sharing [recovery and response](<https://www.ca.gov/LAfires/>) resource information 
  * Avoiding travel to impacted areas
  * Volunteering through [California Volunteers](<https://www.californiavolunteers.ca.gov/wildfire-recovery/>)
  * Additional ways to donate, through [Philanthropy California](<https://www.philanthropyca.org/2025-california-disaster-response>)
