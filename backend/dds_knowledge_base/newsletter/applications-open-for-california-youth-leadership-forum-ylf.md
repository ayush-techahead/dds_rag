# Applications Open for California Youth Leadership Forum (YLF) : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/applications-open-for-california-youth-leadership-forum-ylf/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/applications-open-for-california-youth-leadership-forum-ylf/)

---

The application for the 2025 [California Youth Leadership Forum](<https://urldefense.com/v3/__https:/www.dor.ca.gov/Home/Ylf__;!!Jv0_iA8!tD06-CaMOaoK0-3HUzUQhNw-SzImgYb2ramK9MjiHaVFFPgj8iPqqtj0el0ixgaK6JobT3eBlK3BeLHim8abIJWx8sN5$>) (YLF) is now open! Selected delegates will have the opportunity to spend a week in Sacramento, CA, staying in dorms on a college campus. During this week, they will learn about advocacy and leadership, connect with peers, meet professionals with disabilities, interact with state policymakers, develop goals, and create a leadership development plan for their future. Delegates also will have the chance to build lifelong friendships!

**Apply here:**[YLF 2025 Application](<https://urldefense.com/v3/__https:/forms.office.com/g/8EVH8ZUSQr__;!!Jv0_iA8!tD06-CaMOaoK0-3HUzUQhNw-SzImgYb2ramK9MjiHaVFFPgj8iPqqtj0el0ixgaK6JobT3eBlK3BeLHim8abIHnjwMaY$>)  
**Deadline to apply:** February 28, 2025.

**Eligibility criteria for applicants:**  
• Must be a California resident  
• Must be a sophomore, junior, or senior in high school  
• Must have a 504 plan, Individualized Education Program (IEP), or identify as a student with a disability

Leadership experience is encouraged but not required!

There is **no cost** for delegates to attend YLF. All expenses, including travel, lodging, meals, and any necessary accommodations, are covered by YLF, thanks to our sponsors and partners.

For more details, visit the [YLF Webpage](<https://urldefense.com/v3/__https:/www.dor.ca.gov/Home/Ylf__;!!Jv0_iA8!tD06-CaMOaoK0-3HUzUQhNw-SzImgYb2ramK9MjiHaVFFPgj8iPqqtj0el0ixgaK6JobT3eBlK3BeLHim8abIJWx8sN5$>) on the Department of Rehabilitation’s website. If you have any questions, email YLF at [YLF@dor.ca.gov](<mailto:YLF@dor.ca.gov>).
