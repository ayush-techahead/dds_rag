# CalABLE Launches Mobile App and Expands Language Access : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/calable-launches-mobile-app-and-expands-language-access/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/calable-launches-mobile-app-and-expands-language-access/)

> California’s savings plan for people with disabilities is now even more accessible for individuals and families looking to save for the future while protecting their benefits.

---

[CalABLE](<https://www.calable.ca.gov/>), California’s savings plan for people with disabilities, is even more accessible for individuals and families looking to save for the future while protecting their benefits. 

A new mobile app makes it easier to manage CalABLE accounts from anywhere. Users can check balances, make contributions, transfer funds, and track savings right from their mobile device. The app is available on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

The CalABLE website and app are also now offered in eight additional languages: Spanish, Chinese, French, Italian, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, and Russian. This update helps more families access CalABLE in their preferred language.

* When you open an account, you won’t lose your state or federal disability or Medi-Cal or Medicaid benefits. Your CalABLE account can help you protect your current benefits with a way to save for qualified expenses. Normally, Supplemental Security Income (SSI) limits individuals to no more than $2,000 in savings before affecting their eligibility for payments. However, CalABLE allows individuals with disabilities to save up to $100,000 and still keep their SSI payments.

To learn more or get started, visit [calable.ca.gov](<https://calable.ca.gov/>). That website also links to lots of helpful videos that explain these accounts and how to use them.
