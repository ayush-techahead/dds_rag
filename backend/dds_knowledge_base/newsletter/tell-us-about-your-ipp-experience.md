Did you complete your Individual Program Plan (IPP) using the new standardized format? If yes, we want to know how it went!

## Why take the survey?

  * It only takes a few minutes.

  * Your answers are private.

  * You can tell us what worked and what did not.

## How does your feedback help?

Every answer matters! The more people share, the more we can improve.

## Ways to take the survey:

  * Use the QR code at the end of your IPP

  * Take the [survey online](<https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/KWVMTQ9>)

  * Ask your service coordinator for the link

  * Or call us for a paper copy, at 833-421-0061, and then press option 1

Thank you to everyone who already shared their thoughts. Together, we can make the IPP process better for everyone.

## Questions?

  * Email: [ippsupport@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:ippsupport@dds.ca.gov>)

  * Learn more on the [Individual Program Planning](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/ipp/>) webpage.
