# The Vital Role of the 988 Lifeline : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/the-vital-role-of-the-988-lifeline/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/the-vital-role-of-the-988-lifeline/)

> When you or a loved one face a mental health crisis, having immediate, compassionate support can make all the difference. Available 24/7, the 988 Lifeline plays a critical role in supporting individuals and families through challenging moments.

---

When you or a loved one face a mental health crisis, having immediate, compassionate support can make all the difference. The **[988 Lifeline](<https://988lifeline.org/get-help/>)** plays a critical role in supporting individuals and families through challenging moments.

988 provides 24/7 **free** and confidential support for:

  * People experiencing suicidal, mental health, and/or substance use-related crisis

  * People who are worried about a loved one who may need crisis support

The 988 Lifeline connects callers with trained counselors who offer personalized guidance and reassurance. Knowing you or a loved one can access help at any time, brings peace of mind and strengthens your support network.

**Lifeline Benefits**

988 is more than a crisis lifeline. It improves people’s lives by:

  * Preventing unnecessary emergency room visits by providing de-escalation strategies and directing people to appropriate care services

  * Offering tailored support that honors communication needs and personal circumstances

  * Providing an inclusive and judgement-free zone services for people from all backgrounds.

  * Serving as bridge to long-term resources like mental health programs, therapy, and community-based support.

**The 988 Lifeline offers free, confidential support 24/7**. Services are accessible in English, Spanish, and ASL for Deaf/Hard of Hearing individuals. If you or a family or friend need support, you can **call, text, chat, or videophone 988** to connect with trained counselors and find resources.

988 also serves individuals who are neurodivergent: <https://988lifeline.org/help-yourself/individuals-with-neurodivergence/>
