# Learning Together for American Sign Language Day : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/learning-together-for-american-sign-language-day/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/learning-together-for-american-sign-language-day/)

April 15th was National American Sign Language (ASL) Day! Learn some common phrases with Tatyana and the San Diego Regional Center (SDRC) in their video below. As you watch, you’ll see how to sign:

👋 Hello

🙏 Thank you

❤️ I love you

Learning a few basic signs can make a world of difference in promoting awareness and inclusion. Thank you to SDRC for sharing!
