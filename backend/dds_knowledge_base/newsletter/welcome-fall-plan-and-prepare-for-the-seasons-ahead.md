# Welcome Fall! Plan and Prepare for the Seasons Ahead. : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/welcome-fall-plan-and-prepare-for-the-seasons-ahead/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/welcome-fall-plan-and-prepare-for-the-seasons-ahead/)

> September is Preparedness Month and marks the official start of the Fall season! Now is the perfect time to plan and prepare your families, homes, and communities for any unexpected emergencies.

---

September is [Preparedness Month](<https://www.gov.ca.gov/2025/09/18/governor-newsom-proclaims-preparedness-month/>) and marks the official start of the Fall season! Now is the perfect time to plan and prepare your families, homes, and communities for any unexpected emergencies. You can download our 2025 Fall Emergency Preparedness Bulletin for reminders and tips on:

  * Earthquake Safety with the Great ShakeOut

  * Checking Batteries on Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarms

  * Seasonal Flu and COVID Shots

  * Flood Safety

Translations will soon be made available on the [Emergency Preparedness webpage](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/consumers/emergency-preparedness-consumer>).
