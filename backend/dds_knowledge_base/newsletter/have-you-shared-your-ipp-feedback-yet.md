# Have You Shared Your IPP Feedback Yet? : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/have-you-shared-your-ipp-feedback-yet/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/have-you-shared-your-ipp-feedback-yet/)

> What’s working on your Individual Program Plan (IPP) experience, and what isn’t? Your input provided through a quick, private survey can help shape what comes next. Share your feedback and explore different ways to get involved.

---

Your [Individual Program Plan](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/ipp/>) (IPP) has a standardized format to gather information about your life and goals. In your experience, what’s working with the IPP process? What’s not? We want to hear from you!

By taking a quick and private survey, you can help us continue improving the IPP experience for everyone.

## Different ways to begin the survey:

  * Access the [online survey](<https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/KWVMTQ9>) by:

  * the QR code at the end of your IPP

  * asking your service coordinator for the link

  * for a paper copy, call us at 833-421-0061, and then press option 1

## Want to feel more confident guiding your IPP?

Check out our [person-centered IPP planning video series](<https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLNPprD9qxQvHItxoDvUBaMo2YmDEb2GWR>), where Californians share how they shape their plans.

In this video, you’ll hear how participants found jobs they wanted and made a difference in their communities, thanks to the goals they set during their IPP planning.
