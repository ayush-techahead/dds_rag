# Progress on the Master Plan : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/progress-on-the-master-plan/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/progress-on-the-master-plan/)

---

The [Master Plan for Developmental Services](<https://www.chhs.ca.gov/home/master-plan-for-developmental-services/>) was published at the end of March 2025 and is available in multiple languages. It reflects the voice of our community in about 167 recommendations about how the future of developmental disability services can be better. If you receive regional center services, are a family member of someone who does, or are an organization representing them, a service provider, a state department with a role in one of the recommendations—tell us what you’re doing on those recommendations!

You can submit updates directly into [this document](<https://caldds.sharepoint.com/:w:/s/MPDS/Ecnj29ADABJOrHbZVGbk8hwBcc9KHm8B6Bc1gqnrsdsO8w?e=2aeObi>) on the Master Plan’s website. Everyone can share what they are doing to implement its recommendations, and you can see what other people are doing too. Add your update today!
