# February is Children’s Dental Health Month : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/february-is-childrens-dental-health-month/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/february-is-childrens-dental-health-month/)

> For many children and families in our community, accessing dental care can be challenging. There are no-cost preventive services for eligible Californians, while Smile, California offers easy-to-use tools available to support your child’s dental health.

---

For many children and families in our community, accessing dental care can be challenging. Children’s Dental Health Month is a reminder that regular check-ups help prevent pain and more serious health issues later on.

[Medi-Cal Dental covers preventive services](<https://smilecalifornia.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Flyer-English-09-2019.pdf>) at no cost for eligible children and adults. These may include check-ups, cleanings, X-rays, fluoride treatments, and more. Family-friendly tools, like [storybooks and bilingual resources from Smile, California](<https://smilecalifornia.org/ncdhm/>), can help children feel more prepared for a dental visit.

Need to find dental support services near you? Reach out to your regional center service coordinator for more information.
