# Talent in Every Workplace : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/talent-in-every-workplace/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/talent-in-every-workplace/)

---

Every person has something valuable to offer. When workplaces include people with different abilities and experiences, everyone benefits.

[Governor Gavin Newsom’s Disability Employment Awareness Month proclamation](<https://www.gov.ca.gov/2024/10/03/governor-newsom-proclaims-disability-employment-awareness-month-2024/>) recognizes the many ways people with disabilities strengthen California’s workplaces. It also affirms California’s commitment to make sure everyone has access to good jobs, fair wages, and opportunities to succeed.

Across California, people with intellectual and developmental disabilities are making a difference at work every day. The stories that follow are examples of their talent in action.

  * [Using Artistic Talents to Prepare Californians for Emergencies](<http://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/using-artistic-talents-to-prepare-californians-for-emergencies>)
  * [Opening Pathways to STEM Careers for All](<http://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/opening-pathways-to-stem-careers-for-all>)
  * [Building Opportunities at LEGOLAND](<http://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/building-opportunities-at-legoland>)
