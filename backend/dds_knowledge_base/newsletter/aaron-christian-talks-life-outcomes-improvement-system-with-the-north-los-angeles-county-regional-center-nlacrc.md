What does it really mean to modernize how developmental services work in California?

In a recent episode of [North Los Angeles County Regional Center](<https://www.nlacrc.org/>)’s Voices in Action podcast, Aaron Christian, Chief of Population Risk, Quality Assurance, and Data Operations at the Department, chatted about one of his favorite topics: LOIS.

The [Life Outcomes Improvement System](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/lois/>) (LOIS) is a multi-year effort to replace decades-old technology with a more modern, outcomes-based system. The goal is simple but ambitious. LOIS should be built to:

  * Be centered around the person

  * Support better decision-making and communication

  * Work smoothly for individuals, families, regional centers, and service providers

In the conversation with Angela Pao-Johnson, Director at NLACRC, Aaron shares some of what the Department has learned from engaging with our community at town halls around the state.
