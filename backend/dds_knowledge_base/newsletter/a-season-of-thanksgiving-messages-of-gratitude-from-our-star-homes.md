# A Season of Thanksgiving: Messages of Gratitude from Our STAR Homes : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/a-season-of-thanksgiving-messages-of-gratitude-from-our-star-homes/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/a-season-of-thanksgiving-messages-of-gratitude-from-our-star-homes/)

---

[Stabilization Training Assistance Reintegration](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/service-spotlight-what-is-a-star-home/>) (STAR) homes give people a safe, supportive place to stay when they need extra help during a crisis. This month, residents and staff took a moment to tell us what they’re thankful for.

Their messages are a glimpse of what community and everyday support mean to them.

  * “I’m grateful for my mom and my sisters.” -Resident, Central Valley STAR Home

  * “I’m grateful for this nice house we live in.” -Resident, Central Valley STAR Home

  * “I’m grateful for my room and my favorite music.” -Resident, South STAR Home

  * “I’m grateful for the smiles and progress we see every day.” -Staff member, South STAR Home

  * “I’m thankful for the families who trust us with their loved ones.” -Staff member, South STAR Home

  * “I’m thankful for the strength our individuals show as they work through challenges.” -Staff member, Central Valley STAR Home

We also want to share our appreciation for the residents and staff of [all six STAR homes in California](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/state-facilities/star/>), for building communities that people can rely on.
