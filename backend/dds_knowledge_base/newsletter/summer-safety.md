# Prepare Now for Summer Emergencies : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/summer-safety/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/summer-safety/)

---

We’ve already seen many hot weather days in California this summer and there’s still weeks of heat ahead! Check out the recent Summer Edition of the DDS Emergency Preparedness Bulletin for information and reminders to help you stay safe and healthy. The bulletin has tips and steps to prepare for summer weather, fire season, and water activities. It is posted on the Emergency Preparedness [Consumer](<https://gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.dds.ca.gov%2Fconsumers%2Femergency-preparedness-consumer%2F&data=05%7C02%7Ckrystyne.mccomb%40dds.ca.gov%7C7a3cf6af2ad340ee25fa08dcb24086f8%7C7cfc5487eae84559b408d4e67432d3d2%7C0%7C0%7C638581237715035697%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=WUtCxNHSruMF1q5XuqrR2BUOp%2FWSYOs3tJ8OPR7EKPg%3D&reserved=0>) and [Vendor](<https://gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.dds.ca.gov%2Frc%2Fvendor-provider%2Femergency-preparedness-provider%2F&data=05%7C02%7Ckrystyne.mccomb%40dds.ca.gov%7C7a3cf6af2ad340ee25fa08dcb24086f8%7C7cfc5487eae84559b408d4e67432d3d2%7C0%7C0%7C638581237715041523%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=bp9mZPYDYElTaw4cncQAqyF%2B2Djyt36YYZf81T%2ByovE%3D&reserved=0>) pages. 

Preparing for the unexpected is the best defense in any emergency. The summer heat can present many different challenges. Knowing what to do before something happens allows you to respond quickly. This bulletin is part of DDS’ ongoing effort to alert and educate individuals and direct service providers about specific risks to our community. Do not be caught unprepared. Plan today!
