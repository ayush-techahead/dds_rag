# Explore Community Supports Options in Medi-Cal : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/explore-community-supports-options-in-medi-cal/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/explore-community-supports-options-in-medi-cal/)

---

Did you know that Community Supports often are available through your managed care plan if you are enrolled in Medi-Cal? Community Supports are services designed to address health-related social needs, help people live healthier lives, and avoid higher, costlier levels of care. Some examples are:

  * Air filters and ventilation,
  * Home modifications,
  * Transition assistance from nursing facilities or homelessness,
  * Accessibility improvements,
  * Establishment of utilities

People enrolled in Medi-Cal may receive a Community Supports service from their managed care health plan if they meet the eligibility criteria. Their managed care plan also must determine that the service is a medically appropriate and cost-effective alternative to services covered under the base Medi-Cal program. Look up your county and managed care plan on the Department of Health Care Services (DHCS) [Community Supports Search Tool](<https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/CalAIM/ECM/Pages/services.aspx>) to see what might be available!
