# Discover the Early Childhood & Transition Resource Lookup Map for Families : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/discover-the-early-childhood-transition-resource-lookup-map-for-families/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/discover-the-early-childhood-transition-resource-lookup-map-for-families/)

> The Early Childhood & Transition Resources Lookup is a user-friendly map designed to help families find these critical services for their children. 

---

Navigating early childhood and transition resources just got a little easier! The Department is excited to announce the launch of the [Early Childhood & Transition Resources Lookup](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/early-start/childhood/>). It is a user-friendly, interactive map designed to support families as they access these critical services for their children. We appreciate the input of families to develop this new tool!

**_What does this map offer?_**

It’s a one-stop tool to locate essential early childhood and transition resources in your community. Just add your zip code, address, or city, and you can locate contact details, addresses, and websites for:

  * Regional Centers
  * Family Resource Centers
  * Childcare Providers
  * School Districts
  * Head Start Programs
  * Family Empowerment Centers

In California, [early childhood](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/early-start/>) services are for children from birth to age 3, and transition services are for children ages 3-5. The Early Childhood & Transitions Lookup can help save time and reduce the frustration of searching multiple sites when navigating services and resources for your child.

Explore and share the Early Childhood & Transition Resource Lookup today at [www.dds.ca.gov/childhood](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/childhood>)!
