# Medi-Cal Behavioral Health Student Loan Repayment Program Webinars : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/medi-cal-behavioral-health-student-loan-repayment-program-webinars/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/medi-cal-behavioral-health-student-loan-repayment-program-webinars/)

> Mark your calendars and register to learn how eligible behavioral health practitioners may qualify for educational loan repayment.

---

The California Department of Health Care Access and Information (HCAI) is launching Cycle 2 of the [Medi-Cal Behavioral Health Student Loan Repayment Program (MBH-SLRP),](<https://hcai.ca.gov/workforce/initiatives/behavioral-health-bh-connect/mbhslrp/>) and is hosting two free informational webinars to walk behavioral health professionals through the details. Eligible behavioral health practitioners may qualify for up to $240,000 in educational loan repayment in exchange for a multi-year service commitment at a Medi-Cal safety net setting.

The webinars are scheduled for**Tuesday, April 28, 2026, from 9:30–10:30 a.m. and Thursday, May 7, 2026 from 10:00–11:00 a.m**. You can register for the webinars on the [HCAi website](<https://hcai.ca.gov/workforce/initiatives/behavioral-health-bh-connect/mbhslrp/>). Attendees will learn:

  * how individuals can apply,
  * what service commitments are required,
  * which settings and professions qualify, and
  * how organizations can support staff in taking advantage of this program.

Applications open May 1, 2026. You can register and share this opportunity with your colleagues and networks. For questions, contact [MBHSLRP@hcai.ca.gov](<mailto:MBHSLRP@hcai.ca.gov>).
