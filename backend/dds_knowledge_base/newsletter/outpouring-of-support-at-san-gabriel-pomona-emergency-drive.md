# Outpouring of Support at San Gabriel/Pomona Emergency Drive : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/outpouring-of-support-at-san-gabriel-pomona-emergency-drive/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/outpouring-of-support-at-san-gabriel-pomona-emergency-drive/)

---

Stacks of food, hygiene supplies, diapers, pet supplies, and more poured into the San Gabriel/ Pomona Regional Center (SGPRC) on January 11, 2025, in support of individuals and families impacted by the fires. The regional center held an emergency drive to provide relief to the people they serve who lost their homes or belongings. The event was a success, with the community surpassing all planning expectations! SGPRC also has received more than $42,000 in relief funds and countless heartfelt messages of support. SGPRC is hosting an [Eaton Wildfire Resource & Recovery Fair](<https://www.sgprc.org/about-us/emergency-resources-wildfires>) to share resources for families served by the regional center. Thank you to everyone lifting up and helping Californians facing loss following the devastating wildfires.

Find ways you can help individuals and families impacted in this [list for wildfire response and recovery.](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/january-2025-wildfire-response-and-recovery/>)
