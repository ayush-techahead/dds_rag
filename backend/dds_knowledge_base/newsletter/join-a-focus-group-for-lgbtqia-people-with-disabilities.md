If you have an intellectual or developmental disability and also identify as LGBTQIA+ (lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer, intersex, asexual, or another diverse gender or sexual identity), we encourage you to share your voice.

In the LGBTQIA+ Focus Group, you’ll be able to offer your thoughts, ideas and experiences to help improve programs, services, and support.

### **What is this group about?**

This focus group will meet online and talk about their thoughts, ideas, and experiences. A person will ask questions, and everyone in the group will have a chance to share what they think. 

What you share will help:

  * Learn how to better support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities and LGBTQIA+ identities 

  * Get ideas about how to ask respectful and easy-to-understand questions about sexual orientation and gender identity (also called SOGI) 

  * Improve programs, services, and rules to better meet your needs

### **What you need to know about joining**

  * The group will meet every three months. 

  * Each meeting will last 2 hours, with a 15-minute break in the middle of the meeting. 

  * Meetings will be online using Zoom. 

  * Members will be paid for each meeting.

  * Each member participates for one year. 

  * New members will join later so the focus group can continue.
