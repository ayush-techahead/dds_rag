# Follow DDS on Social Media! : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/follow-dds-on-social-media/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/follow-dds-on-social-media/)

Are you on Instagram, Facebook, X, or YouTube? Follow and subscribe to the Department! You’ll see stories from Californians with intellectual and developmental disabilities and community members, updates from the Department and regional centers, and events happening statewide.

You can:

  * Find us on Instagram with @CalDDS_
  * Follow us on Facebook and X at @CaliforniaDDS
  * Subscribe to the YouTube channel at @CaliforniaDDS
  * Get connected on LinkedIn at @DDSCareers
