# Don’t Miss Our 2026 Career Fair in Sacramento : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/dont-miss-our-2026-career-fair-in-sacramento/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/dont-miss-our-2026-career-fair-in-sacramento/)

> Looking for meaningful work? Join us on April 7 to meet our team, explore open roles, and get help navigating the hiring process. Plan your visit and get ready to take the next step.

---

Are you looking for a career where you can make a difference? Join us at the Department’s annual career fair on April 7, 2026.

Whether you are exploring career opportunities or are ready to apply, this event is a great way to learn about working for the Department and supporting the intellectual and developmental disability (IDD) community.

**Career Fair**

April 7, 2026

10 a.m. – 2 p.m.

Allenby Building Lobby

1215 O Street,

Sacramento CA 95814

[Register Today!](<https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=h1T8fOjqWUW0CNTmdDLT0tBglVRlmE1KlO1KVrxx4_hUMEI2NTVKWEJUMzdJTklURExWUjFESFlHOS4u&route=shorturl>)

Attendees can:

  * Get support navigating the State of California’s hiring process
  * Meet staff from different areas of the Department
  * If you’re a Californian with IDD, Workforce Diversity Specialist Kathryn Jue will partner with you in identifying career opportunities within the Department. If you are a Californian with an intellectual or developmental disability interested in working for the Department, you also can [request a virtual meeting with Kathryn Jue](<https://gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Foutlook.office365.com%2Fbook%2FDDSWorkforceSupportBookings%40dds.ca.gov%2Fs%2FjZzzkRqxPk-TFiWI3f3cHQ2%3Fismsaljsauthenabled&data=05%7C02%7CRosaleen.OrtizdeJesus%40dds.ca.gov%7C089197297ab94629fc4008de7bbba903%7C7cfc5487eae84559b408d4e67432d3d2%7C0%7C0%7C639084243108580072%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=UW7iwfTeAYAQQoSEzBIbCjx%2F3nbvvj71yfqBykLji1M%3D&reserved=0>), to learn about available opportunities.
  * Connect with representatives from the Department of Rehabilitation to learn about the State’s Limited Examination and Appointment Program (LEAP) certification process

The Department offers meaningful work, competitive pay, great benefits, and a defined-benefit pension plan.

## On-Site Interviews

There are a limited number of on-site interview spots available. To be considered, you must complete the [required exam and state application](<https://calcareers.ca.gov/CalHRPublic/Landing/Jobs/Steps.aspx>) BEFORE the event, for these positions:

  * Psychiatric Technicians (all levels)
  * Psychiatric Technician Assistants (Certified Nursing Assistants qualify for this position, too)
  * Behavior Specialists

## Not in Sacramento or can’t attend in person?

Explore [career opportunities](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/careers/>) on our website. If we are not able to interview you at the event, or you cannot attend, you may be invited to interview at a later date.

## Event accessibility

The building is wheelchair-accessible. Computers, and American Sign Language and Spanish interpretation will be available during the event. Please contact outreach@dds.ca.gov if you need additional accommodations to participate.
