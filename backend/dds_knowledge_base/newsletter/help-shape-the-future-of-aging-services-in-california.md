# Help Shape the Future of Aging Services in California : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/help-shape-the-future-of-aging-services-in-california/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/help-shape-the-future-of-aging-services-in-california/)

> The California Department of Aging wants to hear from you! Its Aging and Disability Resource Connection (ADRC) Advisory Committee is looking for new members.

---

The California Department of Aging wants to hear from you! Its [Aging and Disability Resource Connection (ADRC) Advisory Committee](<https://aging.ca.gov/Providers_and_Partners/Aging_and_Disability_Resource_Connection/Advisory_Committee/>) is looking for new members. Lend your voice to improve services and support for older adults and caregivers across the State.

The [ADRC](<https://aging.ca.gov/Providers_and_Partners/Aging_and_Disability_Resource_Connection/>) is an important part of California’s “No Wrong Door” system. It guides older adults, people with disabilities, and caregivers to find information, services, and supports in one place.

The committee is seeking a diverse group of members with experience with long-term services and support (LTSS), including:

  * Experts and service providers

  * People who receive services

  * Family members and caregivers

As an ADRC advisory committee member, you will:

  * Serve a two-year term starting in July 2026

  * Support training, policy development, and program quality

  * Coordinate with community organizations and healthcare partners to improve access to benefits and programs

  * Help raise awareness of the ADRC network

### How to Apply

  * Review the [Announcement Letter](<https://aging.ca.gov/download.ashx?lE0rcNUV0zZSOiHCWdwkfQ%3d%3d>)

  * Complete the [Membership Application](<https://aging.ca.gov/download.ashx?lE0rcNUV0zYTTZIropvAeg%3d%3d>)

  * Email your application to [ADRC@aging.ca.gov](<mailto:ADRC@aging.ca.gov>)

**Applications are due May 4, 2026.**
