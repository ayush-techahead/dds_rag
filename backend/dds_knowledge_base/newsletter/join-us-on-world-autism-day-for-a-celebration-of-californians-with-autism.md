# Join us on World Autism Day for a Celebration of Californians with Autism! : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/join-us-on-world-autism-day-for-a-celebration-of-californians-with-autism/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/join-us-on-world-autism-day-for-a-celebration-of-californians-with-autism/)

---

April is Autism Month! Celebrate with us by lifting community voices and recognizing the strengths and experiences of autistic people across the state.

**World Autism Day Celebration**

Thursday, April 2, 2026

12:00 p.m. – 1:00 p.m.

Virtual via Zoom: [Register today!](<https://cal-dds.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_pqw8afvWQHyLXw11EcffxA#/registration>)

We’ll hear from a panel of advocates on the autism spectrum. They’ll share their stories, insights, and hopes for a more inclusive future. Together, we will discuss challenges, highlight accomplishments, and honor the diverse stories that shape our community. This free event offers a space for connection, reflection, and celebration.

American Sign Language and Spanish interpretation will be available.
