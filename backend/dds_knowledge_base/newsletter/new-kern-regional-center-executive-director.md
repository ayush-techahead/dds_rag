# New Kern Regional Center Executive Director : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-kern-regional-center-executive-director/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-kern-regional-center-executive-director/)

---

The Department would like to congratulate Enrique Roman for his appointment as the new Kern Regional Center (KRC) Executive Director.

![Man wearing glasses and a suit smiles for portrait photo](https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/E.-Roman-Headshot-08-14-23-980x1075-1-250x274.jpg)

Enrique served as interim Executive Director following Dr. Michi Gates, who was appointed as the Department’s Chief Deputy Director by Governor Gavin Newsom. You can read the [KRC Board of Directors’ announcement](<https://kernrc.org/2024/11/01/krc-board-of-directors-appoints-new-executive-director/>) on the KRC website.
