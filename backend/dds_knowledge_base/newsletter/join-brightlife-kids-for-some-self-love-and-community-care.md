# Join BrightLife Kids for some self-love and community care : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/join-brightlife-kids-for-some-self-love-and-community-care/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/join-brightlife-kids-for-some-self-love-and-community-care/)

> Children can build resilience when they learn self-kindness and have access to the right supports in their community. BrightLife Kids has tools to support you and your family build resilience, manage stress, and thrive.

---

Children can build resilience when they learn self-kindness and have access to the right supports in their community. This month, [BrightLife Kids](<https://www.brightlife.kids/>) is sharing two resources that can address both areas of potential growth.

## **The Self-Love Letter Project**

February is often about sharing love with others. BrightLife Kids Self-Love Letter Project invites children to start with themselves. Encouraging children to write a few words of kindness, pride, and care, is a simple way to spark self-esteem and connection at home.

Download and share the [Self-Love Letter Project](<https://assets.ctfassets.net/96gdpqkm7elu/3GttgqFYD6LveRWTdJsh8f/ee9655ded6b56b20852809202c0908bb/The_Self-Love_Letter_Project.zip>) today and help spread confidence and emotional strength in your community.

## **Mental Health Strategies for Black Children and Families**

[Join BrightLife Kids’ free webinar on Black mental health and wellness](<https://hellobrightline.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_7NqaC7t0TGC_EPDIoSjU9w#/registration>) on February 26th, 12:00pm PST. Register today to learn strategies to improve mental health and create lasting change focusing on community strengths and healing practices.

## **Need a helping hand?**

Brightlife Kids offers free behavioral health coaching for California families with kids ages 0–12. No insurance or referral are needed.

[Register](<https://app.hellobrightline.com/sign_up/?locale=en-US&anonymous_id=7118e7cf-7161-459a-b238-a168b91680ec&ours_user_id=657679de-c9e8-4a5a-bec2-9cc6c84b7af2&first_lp=brightlifekids>) for BrightLife Kids today and access free tools to support you and your family build resilience, manage stress, and thrive.
