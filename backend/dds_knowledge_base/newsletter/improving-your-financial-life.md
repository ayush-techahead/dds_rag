# Improving Your Financial Life : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/improving-your-financial-life/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/improving-your-financial-life/)

> Are you employed? Do you have money in the bank? If you have a disability, using a CalABLE account can make your financial life a lot better.

---

Are you employed? Do you have money in the bank? If you have a disability, using a [CalABLE account](<https://www.calable.ca.gov/>) can make your financial life a lot better. A CalABLE account is like a savings account, with benefits:

  * You can spend the money in your account for any purpose related to your disability, from rent to health care.

  * You can save up to $100,000 in a CalABLE account before this money is counted when you seek eligibility for state and federal programs like Supplemental Security Income (SSI) and Medi-Cal.

  * Accounts are available in 20 languages and come with a debit card.

Curious about how you can use and sign up for an account? [Learn how CalABLE works!](<https://calable.ca.gov/how-it-works>)
