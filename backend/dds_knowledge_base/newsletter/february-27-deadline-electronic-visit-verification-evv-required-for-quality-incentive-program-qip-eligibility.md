# February 27 Deadline: Electronic Visit Verification (EVV) Required for Quality Incentive Program (QIP) Eligibility : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/february-27-deadline-electronic-visit-verification-evv-required-for-quality-incentive-program-qip-eligibility/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/february-27-deadline-electronic-visit-verification-evv-required-for-quality-incentive-program-qip-eligibility/)

> Don’t wait to get EVV compliant! To meet the EVV requirement for the QIP, providers must complete one of these following steps by February 27, 2026.

---

Don’t wait to get EVV compliant! Service providers of personal care services and home health services who want to qualify for the Quality Incentive Program (QIP) must complete certain requirements outlined in this [QIP Directive](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/D-2025-QualityIncentiveProgram-015_ProviderEligibility_2.pdf>).

To meet [the EVV requirement](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/EVV_RC_Service_Codes_Guidance.pdf>) for the QIP, providers must complete one of these following steps by February 27, 2026:

  * [Complete EVV self-registration](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Quick_Reference_Guide_CalEVV_Provider_Self-Registration_October2024.pdf>), or

  * Be verified by your regional center as exempt from EVV.

Providers of these services who do not complete one of these steps by February 27, 2026, will not be eligible for the QIP effective July 1, 2026.

## How to Check Your Status

If you are unsure whether you have already self-registered, review the [EVV registration list](<https://view.officeapps.live.com/op/view.aspx?src=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.dds.ca.gov%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F02%2FEVVTracker_Updated_20260202.xlsx&wdOrigin=BROWSELINK>) on our website.
