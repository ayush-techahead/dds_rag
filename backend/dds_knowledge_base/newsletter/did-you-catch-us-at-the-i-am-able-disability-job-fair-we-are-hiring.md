# Did You Catch Us at the I AM ABLE Disability Job Fair? We Are Hiring! : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/did-you-catch-us-at-the-i-am-able-disability-job-fair-we-are-hiring/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/did-you-catch-us-at-the-i-am-able-disability-job-fair-we-are-hiring/)

---

![Two women in a busy room face a table with printed materials on it and a blue tablecloth. A smiling woman is talking to them.](https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/IamAble-1-250x313.jpg)

Department staff greeting job seekers at the I AM ABLE job fair in Sacramento on October 14, 2025.

As part of National Disability Employment Awareness Month, Department staff participated in the I AM ABLE Disability Job Fair in Sacramento on October 14. Hosted by PRIDE Industries, the event brought together more than 40 inclusive employers committed to expanding opportunities for people with disabilities.

We enjoyed meeting with so many job seekers, sharing information about employment supports, and talking about careers with the Department.

We believe diverse talents belong in every workplace – including ours!

Are you interested in joining our team? See all of our job opportunities here: [www.dds.ca.gov/general/careers.](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/careers/>)

In early November, [Kathryn Jue will join the Department](<https://www.gov.ca.gov/2025/10/17/governor-newsom-announces-appointments-30/>), and she will help people navigate the State’s hiring process. Also, check out [www.jobs.ca.gov](<http://www.jobs.ca.gov/>) which has more information and tips to help you learn about and get through the State’s civil service system.

We look forward to talking with you about your future!
