# Watch the New Person-Centered Planning IPP Videos : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/watch-the-new-person-centered-planning-ipp-videos/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/watch-the-new-person-centered-planning-ipp-videos/)

---

There are videos and preparation tools now available about the [new person-centered Individual Program Plan](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/ipp/>) (IPP)! Hear directly from individuals, family members, and professionals about person-centered planning, what it means to them, and helpful tips to get started. These videos, and the “Your Plan” tools, are a great way to prepare for your Individual Program Plan meeting.

In these videos you will find:

  * Examples of people talking about their strengths and what they like about themselves

  * The rights you have when planning your services and supports
  * Stories from people about their experience with person-centered service planning, and what has been important to them
  * Ideas about things to keep in mind when you plan your services and supports
  * Information about each person’s role in the planning process

The people in these videos share their own experiences and ideas on what is possible. We hope the videos help you start thinking of your own life goals, desires, and any supports to fulfill them.

You can view these videos in any order, and at any time. You also can select to view the videos with captions in English or Spanish. Visit the DDS [IPP web](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/ipp/>)page to watch them and find other helpful planning tools, like the Your Plan guide.
