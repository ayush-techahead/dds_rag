# New Autism Resource Hub : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-autism-resource-hub/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-autism-resource-hub/)

---

Check out the new Autism Resource Hub! It’s designed for self-advocates, families, providers, and partners. The Hub includes handouts, links to resources, and tools, like the Autism Helpline. You can use it to learn about autism and the available supports and services for Californians. It also provides links to agencies offering additional services and resources. New tools and information will be added regularly.

The [Autism Services Branch](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/dds-autism-services-branch-celebrates-1-year/>) invites you to visit and share the Autism Resource Hub in your communities and networks. You can find it on the Department’s website’s Initiatives page or by following this link: [**Welcome to the DDS Autism Resource Hub**](<https://gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.dds.ca.gov%2Finitiatives%2Fautism%2F&data=05%7C02%7CKrystyne.McComb%40dds.ca.gov%7Cac36f3636756465ad83e08dcff5fb0e8%7C7cfc5487eae84559b408d4e67432d3d2%7C0%7C0%7C638666033663573095%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=rzCL4RIX%2B2CYT1sfjv%2Bkyax9MIS6sFK%2BbWRuNFzxB24%3D&reserved=0>).
