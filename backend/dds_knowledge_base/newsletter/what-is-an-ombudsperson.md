# What is an Ombudsperson? : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/what-is-an-ombudsperson/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/what-is-an-ombudsperson/)

---

An Ombudsperson is someone who can provide unbiased information, listen to concerns, and help resolve issues. The Department has an Office of the Ombudsperson and an Office of the Self-Determination Program Ombudsperson.

**These Offices can:**

  1. Provide information to regional center clients and their families,
  2. Facilitate resolution to disagreements and complaints,
  3. Make recommendations to DDS and the Legislature, and
  4. Compile and report data.

**These Offices cannot:**

  1. Decide disputes in favor of one party or another,
  2. Represent someone in the appeal process,
  3. Make a recommendation to a court or hearing officer, or
  4. Overturn a final hearing decision. ![Woman with Blazer grins](https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Katie-Hornberger-2-250x250.png)
