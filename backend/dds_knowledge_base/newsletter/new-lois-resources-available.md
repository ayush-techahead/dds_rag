# New LOIS Resources Available! : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-lois-resources-available/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/new-lois-resources-available/)

> New tools are now available to help you better understand the LOIS project and what it means for the future of services and supports. Take a look at what’s been released.

---

Two new [Life Outcomes Improvement System (LOIS)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/lois/>) resources are now available on our website.

Download the documents below for an overview of the project and answers to common questions:

  * [LOIS Project Information Sheet (PDF)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/LOIS-Project-Info-Sheet_20261702.pdf>)

  * [LOIS Project FAQs (PDF)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/LOIS-FAQs-_20261702.pdf>)

LOIS is a multi-year project to replace outdated information systems used by regional centers with a more modern, user-friendly platform. The goal for the new system is to make it easier for Californians with intellectual and developmental disabilities, their families, service providers, and regional center staff to manage services, access information, and communicate.

Questions or comments can be sent to [LOIS@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:LOIS@dds.ca.gov>).
