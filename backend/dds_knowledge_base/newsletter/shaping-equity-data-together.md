# Shaping Equity Data Together : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/shaping-equity-data-together/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/shaping-equity-data-together/)

> Community input is key to make sure the measures we use reflect what matters to the people who rely on these services and supports.

---

A new project is underway to make important equity data easier to find and understand. Community input is key to make sure the measures we use reflect what matters to the people who rely on regional center services and supports.

Your voice matters in shaping what measures are chosen and how we track and share this information! The Department will be seeking community feedback on:

  * Proposed measures
  * How data are displayed
  * The best ways to make the measures are meaningful and useful.

Comments and feedback will be collected during meetings with community groups, webinars, and through email. Dates of events will be posted to the [Events page of the Department’s website](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/dds-events/>). Stay tuned for updates about how you can get involved!
