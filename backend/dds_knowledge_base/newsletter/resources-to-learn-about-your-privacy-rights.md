# Resources to Learn About Your Privacy Rights : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/resources-to-learn-about-your-privacy-rights/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/resources-to-learn-about-your-privacy-rights/)

> It’s important to know about your rights to privacy and how to honor your privacy or that of the people you support.

---

Personal privacy is an important part of all our lives. A new Wellness and Safety Bulletin gives you information about the importance of privacy at home, and how to honor your privacy or the privacy of the people you support.

The guides for individuals and families, and for service providers, include how to discuss personal privacy with the planning team, and what steps to take if someone feels like their privacy rights have been violated.
