# Thank You for Supporting Provider Directory Progress : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/thank-you-for-supporting-provider-directory-progress/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/thank-you-for-supporting-provider-directory-progress/)

---

We’re thrilled to announce that the Provider Directory now includes over 16,500 service providers! Over the last two months alone, 8,511 vendors were added and invited to register their information in the system. This progress would not have been possible without the continued collaboration and support of providers, regional centers, and the community.

To achieve the goal of creating a centralized directory of service provider information, all service providers need to register, submit, and maintain their records. We appreciate your continued help in keeping service providers informed and supporting them to participate in the Provider Directory and earn incentives as a part of the Quality Incentive Program (QIP).

Once completed, this online portal will store and display provider information statewide. It became available for regional centers and service providers in October 2024 to validate basic information. Future phases will improve the quality and content, and eventually focus on making the Provider Directory accessible to individuals and families to locate service providers.
