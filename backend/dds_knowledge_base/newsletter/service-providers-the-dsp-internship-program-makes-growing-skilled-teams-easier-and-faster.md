The Direct Support Professional (DSP) Internship Program connects service providers with interns who are ready to make a difference. Providers get help filling key positions, while interns gain valuable experience and career opportunities.

### What the DSP Internship Program Does for Providers

[All’s WELL Healthcare Services](<https://www.allswell.com/>) (All’s WELL) is contracted to administer the program by recruiting and interviewing potential DSP Interns, facilitate and pay for background checks, provide interactive trainings, and make placement recommendations.

The program is designed to:

  * Help providers recruit and keep quality employees

  * Cover hiring costs such as background checks, health screenings, and initial training

  * Give providers a chance to see interns in action before making a job offer

The DSP Internship Program is a win-win. Don’t just take it from us– read below about what’s worked for other service providers around California.

### Success Story: UCP of Stanislaus County

Chris Martin, Executive Director of [UCP of Stanislaus County](<https://ucpstan.org/>), described the program as “a game-changer”. He identified its benefits:

  * **Access to engaged candidates:** Interns are genuinely interested in the work and often become valued employees.

  * **Zero cost to providers:** It covers background checks, health screenings, and initial training before placement.

  * **Smooth process:** Providers can observe interns in real-world settings before hiring. If an intern isn’t the right fit, All’s WELL helps make an easy transition.

“We highly recommend participating—it’s been a great opportunity to grow our team with dedicated, passionate individuals,” said Chris.

### Success Story: Shield of Dreams

Matt Baker, owner and CEO of [Shield of Dreams](<https://shieldofdreams.com/home>), said “The DSP Internship Program has been crucial to our business.”. He shared how the program has helped his business:

  * **Efficient hiring support:** All’s WELL handles screening, background checks, and drug testing, saving time and money.

  * **High-quality candidates:** “We’ve welcomed 5 interns, hired 3, and the other 2 received job offers elsewhere during their internships—proof of the caliber of candidates in this program.”

  * **Organic team growth:** Hired interns referred family and friends, expanding the team naturally.

  * **Excellent communication:** “We let All’s WELL know the kind of employees we need—and they consistently deliver.”

“We couldn’t be happier with the program and recommend it to all. It’s been an incredible resource for our company’s growth,” said Matt.

### Success Story: A Better Life Together, Inc.

“In today’s challenging hiring environment, this initiative has proven to be a valuable resource in identifying and onboarding quality entry-level candidates,” said Clyde Williams, Director of Operations at [A Better Life Together](<https://www.abetterlifetogether.org/>) (ABLT).

Benefits they’ve seen:

  * **Smooth process:** The coordination process was seamless and well-organized.

  * **Team growth:** The two interns completed the internship and training and joined the ABLT team. Both received positive feedback in their 30- and 60-day evaluations and have expressed satisfaction with their experience so far.

“We are encouraged by these outcomes and look forward to continuing our involvement in this meaningful and impactful program. Thank you for the opportunity to be part of this initiative!” said Clyde.
