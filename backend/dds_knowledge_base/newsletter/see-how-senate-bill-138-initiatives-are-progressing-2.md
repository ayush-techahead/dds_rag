# See How Senate Bill 138 Initiatives are Progressing : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/see-how-senate-bill-138-initiatives-are-progressing-2/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/see-how-senate-bill-138-initiatives-are-progressing-2/)

> This Senate Bill 138 Initiatives summary document describes these law’s requirements and provides an update on the work being done to meet them.

---

New and changed laws in 2023 required statewide standardization of some processes and assessments, and improvements to data collection. This [Senate Bill 138 Initiatives summary](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SB138Summary_September2025.pdf>) document describes these [law’s](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240SB138>) requirements and provides an update on the work being done to meet the requirements. The timeline picture below shows the status of each initiative, including:

  * Generic Services and Common Regional Center Services

  * New and Updated Data Definitions

  * Respite Assessment Template and Processes

  * Intake Processes

  * Vendorization Procedures

  * Individual Program Plan Template and Procedures
