**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/dds-autism-helpline-support-is-a-call-away/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/dds-autism-helpline-support-is-a-call-away/)

> The Autism Helpline can connect you with resources and opportunities to help you and your family reach your goals!

Whether you have questions about diagnosis or supports and services available, the [Autism Helpline](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/autism/>) can connect you to resources and helpful information!

The Department is proud to offer the Autism Helpline, a tool created and operated by the Autism Services Branch, to support the autistic community, families, and caregivers.

Call 833-815-2337 or email [autism@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:autism@dds.ca.gov>) today!
