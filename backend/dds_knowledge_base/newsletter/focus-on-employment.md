## Join an Upcoming Employment Workgroup Meeting

The next Employment Workgroup meeting will be on Wednesday, October 8, from 10:00 a.m. to noon. A link is below to register for this virtual meeting.

The Employment Workgroup started in 2021 to bring in community voices and feedback on employment topics, like one-time employment grants and the Coordinated Career Pathways pilot. Since then, the group has helped the Department by sharing input on many employment-related issues.

At the October 8 meeting:

  * The Department will discuss new employment concepts aimed to create more access to employment supports leading to competitive integrated employment.
  * Workgroup members will give comments and suggestions on different parts of the concept
  * The public can listen in and provide feedback

## The Quality Incentive Program has Employment Measures

The Quality Incentive Program (QIP) offers service providers the opportunity to earn a portion of their rates by participating in various reporting measures. You can [learn more about the QIP on the Department’s website](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/quality-incentive-program/>).

One area of focus for the QIP is employment.

At the QIP workgroup meeting on September 15, 2025, the group reviewed the upcoming employment reporting measure which will affect the rates of employment providers in fiscal year (FY) 2026-27. The reporting measure will ask providers to report on the number and portion of staff who have completed employment specialist training and to report on the number of job placements and milestones achieved by individuals served.
