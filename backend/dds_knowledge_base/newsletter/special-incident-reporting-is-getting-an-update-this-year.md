# Special Incident Reporting is Getting an Update This Year : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/special-incident-reporting-is-getting-an-update-this-year/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/special-incident-reporting-is-getting-an-update-this-year/)

> New reporting requirements will soon take effect for Special Incident Reporting (SIR). Learn about the new resources available to help service providers submit reports about events that impact the health or safety of a person receiving their services.

---

In May 2026, new reporting requirements will take effect for Special Incident Reporting (SIR). Service providers have the responsibility to submit SIRs about events that impact the health or safety of a person receiving their services.

There is a new page on our website with resources on risk management and mitigation to support our community partners in meeting these requirements. Regional centers and service providers can use the following links to learn more and get ready for the updated requirements:

  * [Risk Management & Mitigation](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/risk-management-mitigation/>)

  * [Regional Center SIR Reporting Guidelines](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/RegionalCenterSIRReportingGuidelines.pdf>)

  * [Vendor SIR Reporting Guidelines](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Vendor_ProviderSIRReportingGuidelines.pdf>)

Two webinars were held in early January, for [regional centers](<https://youtu.be/-G02oe1jl-g>) and [service providers](<https://youtu.be/5Q2g_kWSx-s>). Later this Spring, the Department will host a series of trainings for individuals and families, service providers, and regional center staff. These trainings will help with understanding the updates and the available tools and resources.
