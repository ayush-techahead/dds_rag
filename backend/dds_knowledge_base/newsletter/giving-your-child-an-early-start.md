# Giving Your Child an Early Start : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/giving-your-child-an-early-start/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/giving-your-child-an-early-start/)

> When families have the right supports early, children can thrive. Two new video resources can support you in taking steps that can set your children up for success.

---

When families have the right supports early, children can thrive. We’re excited to share two new resources to help families take steps that can set them up for success. 

## **Services for Infants and Toddlers who are Deaf or Hard of Hearing**

We worked with parents and members of the Deaf and Hard of Hearing community to develop a video for families with infants and toddlers who are Deaf and Hard of Hearing. The video provides information about accessing Early Start supports, including connecting with [California’s Early Hearing Detection and Intervention](<https://www.infanthearing.org/states/state_profile.php?state=california>) organization, and other state partners.
