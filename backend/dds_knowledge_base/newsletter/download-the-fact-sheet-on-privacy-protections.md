# Download the Fact Sheet on Privacy Protections : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/download-the-fact-sheet-on-privacy-protections/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/download-the-fact-sheet-on-privacy-protections/)

---

How do the Department and the regional centers use my Personal Information? Will my immigration information be shared with the federal government? Get answers to these questions and more in this simple [Privacy Protection Fact Sheet](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Fact-Sheet-on-Privacy-Protection-Jan-2025-FINAL.pdf>). This fact sheet provides an overview of your legal rights to privacy. The law describes some situations in which your information may be disclosed.

The more detailed [Notice of Privacy Practices](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/DS5310_Consumer_Notice_of_Privacy_Practices_20230905.pdf>) is a complete list of how your information may be used and disclosed, and how you can access your information.
