# Help your Service Provider Join the Provider Directory by May 30 : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/help-your-service-provider-join-the-provider-directory-by-may-30/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/help-your-service-provider-join-the-provider-directory-by-may-30/)

> Service providers must register, login, and submit their data within the Provider Directory by May 30, 2025.

---

We’re thrilled to announce that the Provider Directory now includes over 22,500 vendor numbers! This centralized listing of available services is not possible without the continued collaboration and support of providers, regional centers, and the community.

To complete the goal of creating a centralized directory of service provider information, **all service providers must register, login, and submit their data within the Provider Directory by May 30, 2025.**

_Service providers that do not meet[the deadline](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/D-2025-RateReform-001_RateReform_Implementation_Service_Acknowledgement_Form_Extension_Quality.pdf>) will have their billing suspended beginning July 2025. Access to billing will be restored after a provider submits all their records in the Directory and they are validated by the Department. _

**How you can help:** Please ask your service provider(s) if they have registered in the Provider Directory. You can forward this newsletter to them or direct them to the [Provider Directory](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/provider-directory/>) website.

**Growing the Directory:** Future phases will improve the quality and content and eventually focus on making the Provider Directory available to individuals and families to locate service providers.
