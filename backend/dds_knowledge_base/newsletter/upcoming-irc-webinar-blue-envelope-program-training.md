# Upcoming IRC Webinar: Blue Envelope Program Training : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/upcoming-irc-webinar-blue-envelope-program-training/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/upcoming-irc-webinar-blue-envelope-program-training/)

> The Blue Envelope Program could make a big difference during interactions of people with intellectual and developmental disabilities and police or other first responders. If you’re a service provider, community organization, public safety official, or just someone curious about the program, join this IRC webinar on April 7.

---

[Inland Regional Center (IRC)](<https://www.inlandrc.org/>) will host a webinar on the Blue Envelope Program on April 7, 2026, at 10 a.m.

The Blue Envelope Program is a voluntary way to support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) during interactions with police and other first responders.

The blue envelope can signal that a person may need extra time, accommodation, or support communicating during a traffic stop or other interactions.

In this webinar, participants will learn how the program works and how it can support safer interactions between the IDD community and public safety officials.

The session is open to service providers, community organizations, first responders, and anyone interested in learning more about the program.
