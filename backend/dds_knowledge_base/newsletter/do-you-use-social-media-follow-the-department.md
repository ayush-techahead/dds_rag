# Do You Use Social Media? Follow the Department! : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/do-you-use-social-media-follow-the-department/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/do-you-use-social-media-follow-the-department/)

---

Are you on Facebook, Twitter, or YouTube? Follow and subscribe to the Department of Developmental Services! You’ll see stories from Californians with intellectual and developmental disabilities and community members, updates from the Department and regional centers, and events happening statewide.

You can:

  * Find us on Instagram with @CalDDS_
  * Follow us on Facebook and X at @CaliforniaDDS
  * Subscribe to the YouTube channel at @CaliforniaDDS
