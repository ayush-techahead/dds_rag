# Get Frequent Rate Reform Updates : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/get-frequent-rate-reform-updates/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/get-frequent-rate-reform-updates/)

---

DDS is sending out frequent emails called Rate Reform Highlights. The emails provide updates on rate reform progress for regional centers and service providers. To sign up and receive those updates, please sign up here: [Rate Reform Highlights contact form](<https://lp.constantcontactpages.com/sl/D4adq3J/ProviderDirectory>). The latest edition talks about upcoming directives, incentive opportunities for providers, and how information is being collected for the Provider Directory. You can find all previous editions and other resources on the [rate reform website](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/rate-reform>).
