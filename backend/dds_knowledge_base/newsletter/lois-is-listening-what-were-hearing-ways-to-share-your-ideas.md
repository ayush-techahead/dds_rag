We’ve received a lot of great feedback about LOIS, the Life Outcomes Improvement System! Thank you to everyone for taking the time to learn about the project and sharing your ideas about how the new, online system can best work for you.

Once completed, LOIS will make it easier for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities, families, and service providers to manage services, communicate with regional centers, and access important information, all in one place. It will replace outdated systems with a modern system designed around your needs.

![Man speaks on a phone set on a conference table.](https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/LOIS-townHall2.jpg)

## What we have heard so far

Since our [kickoff webinar](<https://cal-dds.zoom.us/rec/play/lf_2RuqSv4YdwILTOAmWrYYSaktOVoTS3bwvgzAzLyCfE4ePggkvrSlsArxg0whrd__qquXssB4vRwx8.918Phm9EdDNQQZge?eagerLoadZvaPages=sidemenu.billing.plan_management&accessLevel=meeting&canPlayFromShare=true&from=share_recording_detail&startTime=1753914771000&componentName=rec-play&originRequestUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fcal-dds.zoom.us%2Frec%2Fshare%2FbSXG0I4144YyB-HYkxDplk5Ppdvk4r9Nhj5XN3zCXW5uaeihIwR6oz4ykCHvPiM.uTqalhDT55VBIKuJ%3FstartTime%3D1753914771000>) on July 30, we’ve hosted a series of in-person town halls across the State to hear directly from you. We’ve held meetings with individuals and families in Sacramento, Berkeley, Fresno, Pomona, and San Diego, and received many thoughts on our web form and via email. So far, we’ve heard LOIS needs to be flexible, mobile and user-friendly, and connected to real-life needs such as housing, employment, and emergency planning. You’ve also asked for easier ways to update and track things such as your Individual Program Plans (IPPs), service requests, and to share documents before meetings.

Please continue to share your needs and ideas with us! We are reading all the comments received, and they will inform how LOIS is designed.

## Share Ideas Online Anytime

Visit the [LOIS webpage](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/lois/>) and fill out the “Share Your Ideas” form or send your thoughts directly to our team at [LOIS@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:LOIS@dds.ca.gov>).

All community input from the virtual meetings, calls, emails and in-person town halls will be considered. You do not need to submit your input more than once or participate in multiple meetings.

However you choose to share your thoughts with us, we look forward to hearing more about what you want LOIS to do!
