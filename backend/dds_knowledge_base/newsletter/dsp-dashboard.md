_2024 DSP Workforce Survey Complete_  
Thank you to the agencies who participated in this year’s Direct Service Professionals (DSPs) Workforce Survey! The information collected in this survey helps find ways we can work together to improve employment outcomes for DSPs. The survey gathers annual data from agencies employing DSPs. The data collected tells us a lot about the workforce and the things that impact the work of DSPs. Learn more about how to participate on the[ workforce initiatives page of the DDS website.](<http://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/workforce-initiatives>)
