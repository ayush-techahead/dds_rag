# LOIS project to release a Request for Information : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/lois-project-to-release-a-request-for-information/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/lois-project-to-release-a-request-for-information/)

---

In November 2025, the LOIS project will release a Request for Information (RFI). The purpose of the RFI is to improve our understanding of how potential vendors can support the development of a LOIS system for our community. During this RFI process, vendors will have the opportunity to:

  * Ask LOIS project team members questions to better understand what we want LOIS to be able to do for individuals and families, regional centers, service providers, state policymakers, and others.

  * Explain and show how their ideas and tools could meet the LOIS requirements.

  * Estimate potential costs and timelines for what it will take to design and implement LOIS using their proposed tools and services.

The RFI will be available on the LOIS website ([www.dds.ca.gov/lois](<http://www.dds.ca.gov/lois>)) in a few weeks. We are excited to take this next step in the project!
