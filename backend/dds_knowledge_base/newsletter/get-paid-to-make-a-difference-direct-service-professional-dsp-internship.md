# Get Paid to Make a Difference: Direct Service Professional (DSP) Internship : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/get-paid-to-make-a-difference-direct-service-professional-dsp-internship/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/get-paid-to-make-a-difference-direct-service-professional-dsp-internship/)

> The DSP Internship Program is a great opportunity to get hands-on-experience supporting people with intellectual and developmental disabilities alongside a trained DSP.

---

Are you looking for a job where you can earn money, learn valuable skills, and make a difference at the same time? The DSP Internship Program is a great opportunity to get experience directly supporting people with intellectual and developmental disabilities alongside a trained DSP.

No experience? No problem! This paid training program will teach you everything need to know to start a rewarding career. Interested in becoming a DSP intern? Or are you a provider looking to mentor the next generation of DSPs? Let’s connect!

Email [Workforce@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:Workforce@dds.ca.gov>) and [check out the DSP Internship Program](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/workforce-initiatives-4/dsp-internship-program/>) webpage.
