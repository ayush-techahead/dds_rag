# How Federal Changes Will Impact Californians : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/how-federal-changes-will-impact-californians/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/how-federal-changes-will-impact-californians/)

> Following the recent signing of the federal government's budget, California continues its mission to protect the health and well-being of all Californians. A new CalHHS presentation explains how these changes impact Medi-Cal, CalFresh, and Covered California, and what steps the State is taking to move forward. Watch the webinar plus get the California Department of Social Services fact sheet to help communities understand their rights in light of recent immigration raids.

---

Following the recent signing of the [federal government’s budget](<https://conta.cc/46tslrl>), California continues its mission to protect the health and well-being of all Californians. Last week, California Health and Human Services Agency (CalHHS) leaders recorded an important presentation to discuss how these changes will impact Californians. You can watch the presentation, [Navigating Federal Cuts to Health and Human Services in California](<https://youtu.be/cPgC0d_EqUo?si=De7xu2LpoqZNp0JI>), on the CalHHS YouTube. In the video, you’ll hear about:

  * Medi-Cal provisions by Department of Health Care Services

  * Covered California provisions by Covered California

  * CalFresh provisions by California Department of Social Services

  * Next steps by California Health & Human Services Agency Secretary Kim Johnson

In addition, in light of the recent federal immigration enforcement, the California Department of Social Services (CDSS) released a sharable fact sheet for everyone with tips on recognizing and responding to immigration officials:
