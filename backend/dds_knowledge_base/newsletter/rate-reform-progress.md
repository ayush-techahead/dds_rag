# Rate Reform Progress : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/rate-reform-progress/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/rate-reform-progress/)

---

The Department continues to release instructions to regional centers and service providers ahead of the rate reform adjustments that become effective January 1, 2025. [Rate reform](<https://gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.dds.ca.gov%2Frc%2Fvendor-provider%2Frate-reform&data=05%7C02%7CKrystyne.McComb%40dds.ca.gov%7C28cd71c2f6f84e7a729a08dd042a59d0%7C7cfc5487eae84559b408d4e67432d3d2%7C0%7C0%7C638671302125395608%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=I1pfGjUhza1ERbVDDn7cnwRLNZG%2BEX8al5xpuEmbFpY%3D&reserved=0>) in California’s developmental disabilities services system changes the way the State pays for services. Recently released instructions cover Early Start, Employment, Specialized Therapeutic Services, and more. These instructions are available through the [rate reform directives and updates](<https://gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.dds.ca.gov%2Frc%2Fvendor-provider%2Frate-reform%2Fdirectives-updates%2F&data=05%7C02%7CKrystyne.McComb%40dds.ca.gov%7C28cd71c2f6f84e7a729a08dd042a59d0%7C7cfc5487eae84559b408d4e67432d3d2%7C0%7C0%7C638671302125447377%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=ARTh9YBMOv0%2FJC3%2FlK8jfqT4reqd%2F%2FySnhOcCNumWTc%3D&reserved=0>) webpage.

The Department continues to hold Rate Reform Training Webinars to provide overviews of the directives. You can find information about upcoming meetings and watch previous trainings on the [rate reform meeting](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/rate-reform/meetings/>) webpage.
