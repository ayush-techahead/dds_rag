# Standardized Vendor Application Process Update : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/standardized-vendor-application-process-update/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/standardized-vendor-application-process-update/)

> In December 2025, a new standardized vendor application process became available in the Provider Directory. Starting March 1, 2026, all new vendor applications must be completed in the Provider Directory.

---

In December 2025, a new standardized vendor application process became available in the Provider Directory. Service providers must complete this requirement, called vendorization, to deliver services through a regional center. The new process is designed to make the application process easier, more consistent across regional centers, and fully online.

The [Vendorization page on the Department’s website](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/vendorization-process/>) has new tools and resources to support providers through this transition.

What you need to know:

  * Starting March 1, 2026, all new vendor applications must be completed in the Provider Directory. [See the directive](<https://gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.dds.ca.gov%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2025%2F11%2FD-2025-RegionalCenterOperations-004.pdf&data=05%7C02%7CRosaleen.OrtizdeJesus%40dds.ca.gov%7C2a71feb0a9ba4646eee608de7015c068%7C7cfc5487eae84559b408d4e67432d3d2%7C0%7C0%7C639071435920741933%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=ZkLgMGR2twn1hUlZUIodYTpSlhhc9jE%2F2FE2zyCjUWY%3D&reserved=0>) for full details.

  * A [step-by-step video guide](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7BZFeXZNeIE>) is available to walk providers through the new process.

  * A [public graphic](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/OpenVendorizationRequests_20260206.pdf>) now shows pending vendor applications by regional center and by stage of review.

For more details and other helpful materials, visit the [Vendorization page](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/vendorization-process/>).
