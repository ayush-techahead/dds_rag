**What is the Quality Incentive Program (QIP)?**

The QIP is designed to improve outcomes for individuals receiving services, service provider performance, and the quality of services provided. Service providers achieve a portion of their reimbursement rate for participating in quality measures. The measures are designed to set benchmarks for performance in the early phases of the QIP development. These measures reward provider performance and identify opportunities to strengthen and improve the quality of services.

In fiscal year (FY) 2025-26, providers eligible to participate in the QIP will achieve the quality incentive portion of their rate by reporting data to the Department. Data collected will be related to employment, preventative health screenings, service access and workforce capacity, and foundational data related to the provider directory. Detailed information about the measures, eligibility, and data collection will be provided publicly in early summer 2025, after more focus group and full Workgroup meetings.
