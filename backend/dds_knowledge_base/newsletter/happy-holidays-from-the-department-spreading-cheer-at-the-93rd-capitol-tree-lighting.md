# Happy Holidays from the Department: Spreading Cheer at the 93rd Capitol Tree Lighting : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/happy-holidays-from-the-department-spreading-cheer-at-the-93rd-capitol-tree-lighting/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/happy-holidays-from-the-department-spreading-cheer-at-the-93rd-capitol-tree-lighting/)

---

A grand 60-ft tree now illuminates the State’s Capitol Rotunda with the help of nine-year-old Carter Morris of Merced. Carter was selected by the Central Valley Regional Center to join Governor Gavin Newsom and First Partner Jennifer Newsom for the 93rd annual ceremony. You can watch the full tree lighting on the [Governor’s YouTube](<https://www.youtube.com/live/AOYmEhKGayY?si=5hsyW2-hIiE7mcoQ>) page and read more on the [2024 Capitol Tree Lighting](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/general/news-room/capitol-christmas-tree-lighting/>) webpage.

Expanding on four decades of tradition, ornaments made by individuals served by regional centers were hung with care on 6-ft trees representing all 21 regional centers. Check out several of the photos below that captured the magic of the trees and their decorators, which now deck the halls of the Capitol grounds. Happy Holidays to every individual, family, regional center staff, and community partners across the State! We are grateful for you and look forward to continuing our work with you in the new year.
