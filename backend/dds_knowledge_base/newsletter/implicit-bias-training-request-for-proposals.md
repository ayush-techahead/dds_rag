# Implicit Bias Training Request for Proposals : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/implicit-bias-training-request-for-proposals/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/newsletter/implicit-bias-training-request-for-proposals/)

> The Department requires Implicit Bias Training for all Regional Center employees and select contractors. We are now accepting proposals from experts to provide these trainings.

---

The Department requires Implicit Bias Training for all Regional Center employees and select contractors. This training helps enhance service access and equity with developmental services with steps to:

  * Raise awareness of unconscious biases

  * Learn strategies to recognize and address biases

  * Learn how to actively change biases to conduct fair assessments

We are now accepting proposals from experts to provide this training.

Proposals should address the unique needs of our Regional Centers: [Access the Request for Proposal posting](<https://caleprocure.ca.gov/event/4300/HD259003>).
