### Topic Descriptions

  * **All News:** All Department announcements and information impacting our community. This includes the monthly Bridges newsletter!
  * **Directives:** Guidance and instructions on policy issues sent to regional centers.
  * **Children and Youth Services:** Program and service information and resources impacting children and transition age youth who receive regional center services, ages birth to 22, including Early Start and more.
  * **Standardizing Services & Supports:** Progress and next steps for how California is changing the way the state pays for and delivers services, including Rate Reform, Quality Incentive Program (QIP), and Provider Directory (PD) updates.
  * **Regulations Updates:** All rulemaking activity and regulation updates from the Department.
