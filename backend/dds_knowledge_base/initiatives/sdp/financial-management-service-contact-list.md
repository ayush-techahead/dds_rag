_**Please select your Regional Center below to see which FMS providers are currently available.**_ If you would like to work with an FMS provider not listed under your Regional Center, please contact your Regional Center.

More information about the different FMS models can be found in the FMS Model Comparison chart. View the FMS Model Comparison Chart in [English](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/FMSModelsComparisonChart_04272021.pdf>), [Spanish](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/FMS_Model_Comparison_Chart_Spanish.pdf>), [Arabic](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/FMS_Model_Comparison_Chart_Arabic.pdf>), [Hmong](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/FMS_Model_Comparison_Chart_Hmong.pdf>), [Japanese](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/FMS_Model_Comparison_Chart_Japanese.pdf>), [Korean](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/FMS_Model_Comparison_Chart_Korean.pdf>), [Laotian](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/FMS_Model_Comparison_Chart_Laotian.pdf>), [Russian](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/FMS_Model_Comparison_Chart_Russian.pdf>), [Simplified Chinese](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/FMS_Model_Comparison_Chart_Simplified_Chinese.pdf>), [Traditional Chinese](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/FMS_Model_Comparison_Chart_Traditional_Chinese.pdf>), [Tagalog](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/FMS_Model_Comparison_Chart_Tagalog.pdf>), or [Vietnamese](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/FMS_Model_Comparison_Chart_Vietnamese.pdf>)

The roles and requirements for the FMS provider can be found in the Financial Management Service Information directive. To view this directive in English and other languages, visit the [Program Directives page](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/sdp/program-directives/>), find the drop down for “Financial Management Services – December 2018” and select your preferred language.

On April 25, 2024 the Department released guidance regarding [Employer Burden and Other Employment Related Costs](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Self-Determination-Program-Employer-Burden-and-Other-Employment-Related-Costs.pdf>). A summary of the approved employer burdens by FMS are available in [Excel](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Summary_of_Approved_FMS_Employer_Burden_20260104.xlsx>) or [PDF](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Summary_of_Approved_FMS_Employer_Burden_20260104.pdf>).
