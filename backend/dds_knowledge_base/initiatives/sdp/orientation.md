# Orientation : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/sdp/orientation/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/sdp/orientation/)

---

Individuals who want to receive their regional center services in the Self-Determination Program (SDP) must take an SDP Orientation. Beginning April 1, 2026, the California State Council on Developmental Disabilities (SCDD) is the only approved provider to offer the SDP Orientation statewide. To take the SDP Orientation through SCDD, you can [sign up here.](<https://scdd.ca.gov/sdp-orientation/>)

SCDD SDP Orientation webpage: <https://scdd.ca.gov/sdp-Orientation/>
