# Newsletters : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/sdp/newsletters/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/sdp/newsletters/)

---

The Department has developed a Self-Determination Program (SDP) Newsletter designed to provide information on various topics as the program is implemented throughout the state. Each Newsletter will feature updates on the program, a question of the day and feature story focusing on the people and the principles of Self-Determination.

Newsletters will be posted on a regular basis. If you want to be notified of updates, please email [sdp@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:sdp@dds.ca.gov>).
