# Water Safety for ALL Californians : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/water-safety/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/water-safety/)

---

Swimming and recreational water activities are a great way to enjoy the Golden Coast during the warmer weather months at the beach, in pools, rivers, or other waters. These activities provide physical and mental health benefits but also increase the risk of preventable drowning tragedies.

Take steps now to learn drowning prevention techniques and to share water safety information with your communities. Your action can help prevent water-related death and injury and help children and individuals of all ages enjoy California’s waterways safely and responsibly.

Victims of near drowning accidents often experience disabilities for the remainder of their lives. These include central nervous system damage contributing to varying levels of brain damage, resulting in loss of memory, seizures, learning disabilities, paralysis, and sometimes coma. The surviving victims may also experience a lifetime of multiple medical problems including respiratory, cardiovascular, and neuromuscular disorders.

California Laws

California remains committed to decreasing the number of drowning deaths and non-fatal drownings, recently enacting new legislation and initiatives aimed at increasing water safety awareness and practices. [The Neng Thao Drowning Prevention Safety Act](<https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202320240ab1445>), established by Assembly Bill 1445 (2023), and strengthened by Assembly Bill 1005 (2025), authorizes drowning prevention organizations to provide educational materials to public schools. These materials explain why water safety matters, highlight local swimming programs, and share how to access water safety courses. Schools are required to distribute educational materials, selected from an approved list of existing curriculums by the Department of Education, to parents or guardians of students in all grade levels.
