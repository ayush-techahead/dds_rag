### Why does LOIS matter to me?

LOIS should work for YOU. It will be a secure and accessible tool to improve how you talk and engage with your regional center and providers.

### What we’ve heard from the community

The Department held several in-person town halls across the State through October 2025 to gather input from the individuals and families getting regional center services. You can watch the Department’s webinar to get a [summary of the community’s input](<https://cal-dds.zoom.us/rec/share/PULcPaD4gxCaSqLkxjv6RfyYqJS-BHOIW_My1SfYeE8cHe2-HtnZ0MMhflRUm_5U.6Is0FA6n9HnbK42U?startTime=1761861622000>).

### Why does LOIS matter for Service Providers?

LOIS will better support Service Providers by providing streamlined payments, reporting options, communication and more.

### What we’ve heard from Service Providers

The Department hosted several virtual town halls with service providers through October 2025.
