# Standardizing Regional Center Procedures : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/standardizing-regional-center-procedures/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/standardizing-regional-center-procedures/)

---

Senate Bill 138 directs the Department of Developmental Services to standardize certain regional center procedures to make the experience more equal for people across the state. This [Senate Bill 138 Initiatives summary](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/SB138_Summary_202603.pdf>) document describes the [law’s](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240SB138>) requirements and provides an update on the work being done to meet the requirements. The timeline picture below shows the status of each initiative.

Generic Services and Common Regional Center Services (In development)

WIC [4435.2](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4435.2.&lawCode=WIC>) tells the Department of Developmental Services (Department) to:

  1. Provide a definition of generic services, identify barriers to individuals using generic services, describe what regional centers do to help people get generic services, and identify ways to help more. This must be done by July 1, 2025 with community input.
  2. Review whether the new electronic records management system can track generic services that individuals use.
  3. Do an evaluation of the regional center services individuals most often use, and find out if they all are available across the state. Make recommendations for making their availability more equal.
  4. An update must be provided to the Legislature by January 10, 2025.

**In development**

New and Updated Data Definitions (Implemented January 2025)

WIC [4435.1(b)](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4435.1.&lawCode=WIC>) tells the Department to:

  1. Create a standard definitions and individual choices for race, ethnicity and language so that our data can be more useful.
  2. All regional centers must use these new definitions and individual choices to record data beginning January 1, 2025.

Respite Assessment Template and Processes (In development)

WIC [4435.1(c)](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4435.1.&lawCode=WIC>) tells the Department to:

  1. Create one way for all regional centers to review an individual’s need for respite services. This must be done by June 30, 2025.
  2. All regional centers must begin using the new way on January 1, 2026.

**In development**  
The Department plans to finalize the tool, train regional centers how to use it, and provide information to the community about the new respite assessment and what to expect, later in 2026.  
[Standardized In-Home Respite Tool](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/initiatives/standardizing-regional-center-procedures/standardized-respite-tool-public-feedback/>)

Intake Processes (In development)

WIC [4435.1(f)](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4435.1.&lawCode=WIC>) and WIC [4642(a)](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4642.&lawCode=WIC>) tell the Department to:

  1. Create one way for individuals to find out if they are eligible for regional center services. This must be done by January 1, 2025.
  2. All regional centers must report how many individuals went through the process and how long it took them. This begins June 30, 2025 and must be done every three months.
  3. The new electronic records management system must be able to include certain information about these individuals.

**In development**

Vendorization Procedures (Implemented December 2025)

WIC [4435.1(e)](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4435.1.&lawCode=WIC>) tells the Department to:

  1. Create one way for new service providers to become vendors of the regional centers. This must be defined by June 30, 2025.
  2. All regional centers must use the new way beginning January 1, 2026. They must provide an updated list of their service providers every 3 months.

**Vendorization process launched December 2025**  
The standardized vendorization process was launched on December 3, 2025 in the Provider Directory. The implementation plan along with helpful resources for regional centers and service providers can be found on the [Vendorization Process webpage](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/vendorization-process/>).

Individual Program Plan (IPP) Template and Procedures (Implemented January 2025)

WIC [4435.1(d)](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4435.1.&lawCode=WIC>) tells the Department to:

  1. Create one way to make an individual program plan (IPP) and provide directions. This must be done by June 30, 2024.
  2. All regional centers must use the new IPP beginning January 1, 2025.
  3. The new electronic records management system must be able to include information from the IPP.

**A standardized Individual Program Plan template and procedures were established in June 2024 and are currently in use.**
