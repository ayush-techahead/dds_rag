## Membership

The California Department of Developmental Services (DDS) and California Health and Human Services Agency have announced that a total of 157 individuals from across the state have been selected as members of the Developmental Services (DS) Task Force. The DS Task Force provides guidance on the delivery of services to Californians who have intellectual and developmental disabilities.

Forty-two individuals will serve on the Task Force, and an additional 115 people will serve on the Task Force’s five workgroups. In the past, 21 individuals served on the Task Force and workgroups combined.

  * [DS Task Force Membership](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/DSTF_Members_01072021.pdf>)
  * [Community Resources Workgroup Membership](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/DSTF_CommunityResources_01072021.pdf>)  
The Community Resources Workgroup will discuss service development needs and opportunities, including housing, employment, workforce development, health, wellness, and technology.
  * [Oversight, Accountability and Transparency Workgroup Membership](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/DSTF_OAT_01082021.pdf>)  
The Oversight, Accountability and Transparency Workgroup will review and explore ways to measure and monitor service and quality outcomes and enhance transparency.
  * [Safety Net Workgroup Membership](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/DSTF_SafetyNet_01082021.pdf>)  
The Safety Net Workgroup will continue evaluation of, and make recommendations on, the developmental services safety net, to include crisis services through person-centered approaches.
  * [Service Access and Equity Workgroup Membership ](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/DSTF_ServiceAccessEquity_01082021.pdf>)  
The Service Access and Equity Workgroup will continue discussions on creating a culturally and linguistically competent service system, review funded disparity projects and outcomes, explore statewide strategies to improve access, and make data easy to understand for community discussions.
  * [System and Fiscal Reform Workgroup Membership](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/DSTF_SystemFiscalReform_01082021.pdf>)  
The System and Fiscal Reform Workgroup will discuss system and fiscal reforms to better serve individuals, evaluate compliance with federal rules and discuss key system performance outcomes and measures
