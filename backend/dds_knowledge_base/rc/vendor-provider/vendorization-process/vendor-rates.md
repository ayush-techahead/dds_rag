  * Financial Management Services, FEA – Service Code 490 – Effective January 1, 2025, the rate is $160 per individual per month.
  * Financial Management Services, Co-Employer – Service Code 491 – Effective January 1, 2025, the rate is $265 per individual per month.

### **Medi-Cal**

California Department of Health Care Services’ [Medi-Cal Schedule of Maximum Allowances](<https://mcweb.apps.prd.cammis.medi-cal.ca.gov/>) (SMA). Regulations require regional centers to reimburse providers of services listed in the schedule at rates no higher than the rates specified in the SMA.
