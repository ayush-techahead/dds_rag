# Rates Only Files Updated - September 4, 2025 : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/rate-reform/rates-only-files-updated-september-4-2025/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/rate-reform/rates-only-files-updated-september-4-2025/)

---

Rates for services that are a part of rate reform can be found below in the following two documents by regional center:

  * Complete Rate Models as of 1/1/2025 (updated 1/8/25)
  * Rates Only – Effective 1/1/25 _(updated 9/4/25)_

The “Complete Rate Models” pdf provides detailed information on rates by service code. This version is as of January 8, 2025.

The “Rates Only – Effective 1/1/25” excel file provides the full rate as of January 1, 2025. It also includes the base rate (90% of the full rate) and QIP (10 % of the full rate). The full rate includes updated state minimum wage and IRS mileage for 2025. These files were updated September 4, 2025.

**As of 1/1/25 the following information is applicable to Residential services:**

  * Effective January 1, 2025 B&C (SSI/SSP) is $1420.07 and P&I is $179.00
