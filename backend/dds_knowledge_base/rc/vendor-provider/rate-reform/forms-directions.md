## Rates for Providers Serving Consumers from Multiple Regional Centers

Providers may have multiple rates for the same service when services are provided in multiple regional center catchment areas. The [Rates for Providers Serving Consumers from Multiple Regional Center document](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Rates_for_Providers_Serving_Consumers_from_Multiple_RCs_05092022.pdf>) identifies which service codes use rates from the user or vendoring regional center.

## Rate Review Process

Providers who believe there is an error in the rate calculation may submit the rate review form to their Regional Center no later than 60 days after receiving their rates.

  * [Rate Adjustment Effective April 1, 2022 – Rate Review Process](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Rate_Adjustment_Effective_April_1_2022_Rate_Review_Process.pdf>)
  * [Rate Adjustment Effective January 1, 2023 – Rate Review Request Form](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/RATE_REVIEW_FORM_EFFECTIVE_January_1_2023.pdf>)

## Service Detail Collection Forms

If you have received a Service Detail Collection Form and have questions about how to complete it, please refer to the following [instructions](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Instructions_CompletingServiceDetailCollectionForms.pdf>). Submit your completed worksheet for existing vendorizations to your Regional Center.
