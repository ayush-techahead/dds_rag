# Rate Changes : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/rate-changes/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/rate-changes/)

---

## Sick Leave (SB 616)

Senate Bill 616 requires employers with employees who work on or after January 1, 2024, to increase the sick leave accrual rate for these providers to 40 hours or 5 days in each year of employment.[Learn More](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/rate-changes/rate-changes-due-to-sb-616-employee-sick-leave/> "Learn More About Sick Leave \(SB 616\)")

## Rate Study Implementation

Welfare and Institutions Code section 4519.10 added in 2021 and updated in 2022 specifies the timeline for implementation of fully funded rate models for each provider type by July 1, 2024.[Learn More](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/rate-study-implementation/> "Learn More About Rate Study Implementation")

## Assembly Bill 79

Chapter 11, Statutes of 2020, Assembly Bill 79, provided the Department of Developmental Services (DDS) with time-limited funding for rate increases for specified service codes effective January 1, 2021 through December 31, 2021.[Learn More](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/rate-changes/ab79-rate-changes/> "Learn More About Assembly Bill 79")

## Senate Bill 81

Chapter 28, Statutes of 2019, Senate Bill 81, provided the Department of Developmental Services (DDS) with time-limited funding for rate increases for specified service codes effective January 1, 2020 through December 31, 2021.[Learn More](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/rate-changes/supplemental-rate-changes/ > "Learn More About Senate Bill 81")

## Bridge Funding

The 2018 Budget Act provided the Department of Developmental Services (DDS) with one-time funding for service provider rate increases.[Learn More](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/bridge-funding/> "Learn More About Bridge Funding")

## ABX 2-1

Rate increase, effective July 1, 2016, for the purpose of enhancing wages and benefits for staff who spend at least 75 percent of their time providing direct services to consumers.[Learn More](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/abx2-1/> "Learn More About ABX 2-1")

## Sick Leave

Assembly Bill 1522 required employers with employees who work on or after July 1, 2015 and work 30 or more days within a year to provide sick leave at a rate of one hour for every 30 hours worked.[Learn More](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/rate-changes/rate-changes-due-to-ab-1522-employee-sick-leave/> "Learn More About Sick Leave")

## 5.82 Percent Rate Increase

On February 13, 2017, the Sacramento County Superior Court ruled the Department must implement the 5.82 percent rate increase for in-home respite agency, personal assistance and supported living services.[Learn More](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/vendor-provider/5-82-percent-rate-increase/> "Learn More About 5.82 Percent Rate Increase")
