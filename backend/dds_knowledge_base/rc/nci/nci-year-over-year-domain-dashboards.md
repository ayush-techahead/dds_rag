## National Core Indicator Survey Data for the State of California

The dashboard below displays data for key areas of interest collected during each year the surveys were administered. Use the tabs at the top of the dashboard to select the survey (Child Family Survey, Adult Family Survey, Family Guardian Survey, or In-Person Survey). Use the “Select Regional Center” filter to display the data for the regional center of interest or to display the data collected statewide.
