# NCI Comprehensive Dashboards : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/nci/nci-interactive-dashboard/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/nci/nci-interactive-dashboard/)

---

The National Core Indicators (NCI) Surveys are used by the California Department of Developmental Services to assess performance in the services and supports provided to people with intellectual/developmental disabilities (I/DD). These dashboards show data collected in California over the past several fiscal years. To get started, choose a survey to view the results. Due to the large amount of data displayed, please expect to wait up to 60 seconds for each dashboard to load.
