# Lanterman Act Eligibility and Service Appeals Data : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/dashboard/appeals-data/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/dashboard/appeals-data/)

---

The Lanterman Act Eligibility & Service Appeals process is used when individuals want to appeal a regional center’s decision that they are not eligible for Lanterman Act services, or to appeal a regional center’s decision about the type or amount of services. Appeal requests and resolutions are monitored by the Department of Developmental Services.
