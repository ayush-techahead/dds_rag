# Purchase of Service Report : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/dashboard/purchase-of-service-report/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/dashboard/purchase-of-service-report/)

---

Welfare & Institutions Code, Section 4519.5, requires DDS and regional centers to annually collaborate to compile data relating to purchase of service authorization, utilization and expenditures.

To view Regional Center Annual Purchase of Service Reports, please visit the [Purchase of Service Annual Reports page](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/disparities/data/purchase-of-service-annual-reports/>).
