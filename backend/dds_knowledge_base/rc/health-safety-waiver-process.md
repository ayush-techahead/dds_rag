# Health or Safety Waiver Process : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/health-safety-waiver-process/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/health-safety-waiver-process/)

---

Welfare & Institutions Code (WIC) sections [4681.6](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4681.6.&lawCode=WIC>), [4648.4(b)](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4648.4.&lawCode=WIC>), [4681.5](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4681.5.&lawCode=WIC>), [4684.55](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4684.55.&lawCode=WIC>), [4689.8](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4689.8.&lawCode=WIC>), [4691.6,](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4691.6.&lawCode=WIC>) and [4691.9](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=4691.9.&lawCode=WIC>) authorize the Department to approve waivers to rate freezes for the purpose of mitigating health or safety (HS) risks to individuals. Requests for approval of a HS Waiver will be considered only on the basis of the needs of an individual.

The Department is committed to processing HS Waiver requests in a timely manner. When seeking a HS Waiver, regional centers should submit the request to the Department as soon as possible. The following documentation must be submitted in the request:

  1. A complete and accurate written request signed by the regional center Executive Director justifying the need for a rate increase to protect the individual’s health or safety. The regional center must thoroughly review information provided by a vendor, to support the request, prior to submitting to the Department.
  2. A completed HS Worksheet.
  3. A copy of vendor’s original request to regional center.
  4. Any additional documents, as outlined on page two of the directive on HS Waivers.
