# Regional Center Performance Contracts : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/disparities/data/regional-center-performance-contracts/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/disparities/data/regional-center-performance-contracts/)

---

DDS establishes contracts with regional centers that include specific, measurable, performance objectives, which are reviewed by the public on an annual basis. The annual performance contracts are designed to help consumers achieve quality of life, reach meaningful progress above current baselines, and developed services and supports to meet consumer’s needs. Every year, DDS reviews each regional center’s performance data for compliance with their contracts.
