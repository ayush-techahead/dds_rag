# Family Resource Centers : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/disparities/community-navigator-program/family-resource-center-listings/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/disparities/community-navigator-program/family-resource-center-listings/)

---

The listings on this page include the 21 Family Resource Center (FRC) lead organizations contracted directly with the Family Resource Centers Network of California. FRC leads are the main point of contact for the Community Navigator Program in each regional center catchment area across California. FRC leads are listed in alphabetical order by regional center service area.
