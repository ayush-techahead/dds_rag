# Office of Administrative Hearings Advisory Committee (OAHAC) : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/office-of-administrative-hearings-advisory-committee-oahac/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/office-of-administrative-hearings-advisory-committee-oahac/)

---

**Office of Administrative Hearings Advisory Committee (OAHAC):** This committee will meet at least two times per year to give the Office of Administrative Hearings (OAH) and DDS input on conducting the mediations and hearings.
