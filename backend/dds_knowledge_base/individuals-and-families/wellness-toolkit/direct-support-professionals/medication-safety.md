# Medication Safety : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/wellness-toolkit/direct-support-professionals/medication-safety/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/wellness-toolkit/direct-support-professionals/medication-safety/)

---

Follow the Seven Rights when you are administering medication to the individuals you support: Right Person, Right Medication, Right Dose, Right Time, Right Route, Right Reason, and Right Documentation.
