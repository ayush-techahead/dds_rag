# Emergency Preparedness : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/wellness-toolkit/direct-support-professionals/emergency-preparedness/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/wellness-toolkit/direct-support-professionals/emergency-preparedness/)

---

Being prepared for emergencies is the best way to keep you and the individuals you support safe. This poster provides four actions to take to prepare yourself and the individuals you support for an emergency: inform, prepare, create, and practice.
