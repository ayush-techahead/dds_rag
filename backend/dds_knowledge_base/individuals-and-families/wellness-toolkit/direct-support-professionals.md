# Direct Support Professionals : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/wellness-toolkit/direct-support-professionals/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/wellness-toolkit/direct-support-professionals/)

---

A “Direct Support Professional” or DSP works directly with people with intellectual or developmental disabilities to assist the people they work with to become independent and integrated into their community. Here DSPs can access resources to help them mitigate risk and keep the people they support safe.

**Check out each of the topics below for helpful information. If you are interested in more materials that may be helpful, you can check out materials on the[“Vendors/Providers” tab](</consumers/wellness-toolkit/vendors-providers>).**
