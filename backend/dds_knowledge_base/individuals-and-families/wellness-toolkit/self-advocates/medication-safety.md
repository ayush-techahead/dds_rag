# Medication Safety : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/wellness-toolkit/self-advocates/medication-safety/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/wellness-toolkit/self-advocates/medication-safety/)

---

Medications are no joke! If you take your own medication (self-administration), it’s very important to make sure you do it safely. You can work with your support staff or family to make sure that you follow the “seven rights” every time you take your medicine.

This video was filmed during the COVID-19 outbreak and shelter-in-place order.
