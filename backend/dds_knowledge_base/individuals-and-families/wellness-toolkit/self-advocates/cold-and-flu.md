# Protect Yourself from the Cold or Flu : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/wellness-toolkit/self-advocates/cold-and-flu/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/wellness-toolkit/self-advocates/cold-and-flu/)

---

This video provides information for self-advocates on how to keep from getting a cold or the flu. It is important to stay healthy by eating well, washing your hands, and getting a flu shot every year.
