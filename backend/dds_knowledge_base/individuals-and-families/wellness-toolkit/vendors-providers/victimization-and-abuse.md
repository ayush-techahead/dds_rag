## Conversation Guide for Talking About Abuse

This tool provides quick tips and sample questions for Direct Support Professionals (DSPs) to ask when talking about abuse with an individual they support.

  * **View Tool:** [English](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Abuse_Vend_ConversationGuide_english.pdf>) | [Spanish](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Abuse_Vend_ConversationGuide_spanish.pdf>) | [Tagalog](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Abuse_Vend_ConversationGuide_tagalog.pdf>)

## Sexual Abuse and Individuals with Developmental Disabilities

This tip sheet discusses the concepts of privacy and consent, as well as how DSPs can better support individuals by recognizing and reporting sexual abuse.

  * **View Fact Sheet:** [English](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Abuse_Vend_SexualAbuse_english.pdf>) | [Spanish](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Abuse_Vend_SexualAbuse_spanish.pdf>) | [Tagalog](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Abuse_Vend_SexualAbuse_tagalog.pdf>)

## Neglect as a Type of Abuse

Much of the neglect faced by people with developmental disabilities is hidden. Using this tip sheet, DSPs can be aware of what neglect looks like, as well as what to do to prevent that abuse for the individuals they support.

  * **View Fact Sheet:** [English](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Abuse_Vend_NeglectFactSheet_english.pdf>) | [Spanish](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Abuse_Vend_NeglectFactSheet_spanish.pdf>) | [Tagalog](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Abuse_Vend_NeglectFactSheet_tagalog.pdf>)

## Supporting Boundaries

This activity provides guidelines for DSPs and the individuals they support as they identify, and work within their professional and personal boundaries. This activity includes a modified person-centered profile.
