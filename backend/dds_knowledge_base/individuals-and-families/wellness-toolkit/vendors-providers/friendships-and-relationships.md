## What Do I Like to Do?

A great way to begin making friends is to be part of a group that shares your interests. Using this brainstorming tool, DSPs can help the people they support find enjoyable activities through Internet search and by exploring places in the community.

  * **View Tool:** [English](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/FriendshipsRelationships_Vend_WhatdoIliketoDo_english.pdf>) | [Spanish](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/FriendshipsRelationships_Vend_WhatdoIliketoDo_spanish.pdf>) | [Tagalog](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/FriendshipsRelationships_Vend_WhatdoIliketoDo_tagalog.pdf>)

## Five Steps to New Making Friends

This brief tip sheet presents five short steps to making friends, and it can be provided to DSPs so that they can discuss these steps with the individuals they support.

  * **View Tip Sheet:** [English](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/FriendshipsRelationships_Vend_5StepstoMakeNewFriends_english.pdf>) | [Spanish](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/FriendshipsRelationships_Vend_5StepstoMakeNewFriends_spanish.pdf>) | [Tagalog](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/FriendshipsRelationships_Vend_5StepstoMakeNewFriends_tagalog.pdf>)

## Making New Friends

Many people are shy or unsure of how to approach a new person. They might lack confidence, because they don’t know what to do or say. DSPs can use this role-playing activity with the individuals they support to practice meeting new people to build up confidence.

  * **View Activity:** [English](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/FriendshipsRelationships_Vend_MeetingFriendsActivity_english.pdf>) | [Spanish](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/FriendshipsRelationships_Vend_MeetingFriendsActivity_spanish.pdf>) | [Tagalog](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/FriendshipsRelationships_Vend_MeetingFriendsActivity_tagalog.pdf>)

## Talking about Close Relationships

DSPs can help the individuals they support understand how to navigate forming healthy close relationships with others. This tip sheet includes information about having the “talk,” information about online dating, and dating do’s and don’ts.
