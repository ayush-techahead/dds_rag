## Healthy Eating for a Healthy Lifestyle 

This slide deck and companion one-page tip sheet can be used for training DSPs about one of the two parts of a healthy lifestyle: healthy eating. DSPs could also use these resources to talk about healthy eating with the individuals they support.

## My Plan for Getting Active

This activity can be distributed to DSPs to be used to help the individuals they serve find manageable goals and track their progress towards their weekly goal.

  * **View Activity:** [English](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HealthyActive_Vend_PlanForGettingActive_english.pdf>) | [Spanish](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HealthyActive_Vend_PlanForGettingActive_spanish.pdf>) | [Tagalog](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HealthyActive_Vend_PlanForGettingActive_tagalog.pdf>)

## Getting to Know the Food Groups

This fact sheet can help DSPs and self-advocates learn about the five main food groups that are key to a healthy diet. These can be used to create a healthy eating plan.

  * **View Fact Sheet:** [English](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HealthyActive_Vend_FoodGroups_english.pdf>) | [Spanish](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HealthyActive_Vend_FoodGroups_spanish.pdf>) | [Tagalog](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HealthyActive_Vend_FoodGroups_tagalog.pdf>)

## Healthy Eating Habits

This tip sheet introduces some healthy eating habits that DSPs can discuss with and encourage the individuals they serve to select a few habits to try.

  * **View Tip Sheet:** [English](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HealthyActive_Vend_HealthyHabits_english.pdf>) | [Spanish](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HealthyActive_Vend_HealthyHabits_spanish.pdf>) | [Tagalog](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/HealthyActive_Vend-HealthyHabits_tagalog.pdf>)
