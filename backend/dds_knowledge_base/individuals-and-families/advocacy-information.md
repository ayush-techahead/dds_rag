# Advocacy Information : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/advocacy-information/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/individuals-and-families/advocacy-information/)

---

Advocates are trained individuals who help consumers, families, and professionals understand the rights of individuals with developmental disabilities. They provide education and training, and advisory services.

## State Council on Developmental Disabilities

The State Council on Developmental Disabilities is established by state and federal law as an independent state agency to plan for and ensure that individuals with developmental disabilities and their families receive the services and supports they need.

  * [State Council on Developmental Disabilities](<http://www.scdd.ca.gov/>)
  * [SCDD Regional Offices](<https://scdd.ca.gov/>)

## Regional Center and Developmental Center Clients’ Rights Advocates

Developmental centers and regional centers have clients’ rights advocates who are not employees of the centers. They are available to help consumers with their concerns.

  * [Regional Center Clients’ Rights Advocates](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/CC_RCClientRightsAdvocates_20190319.pdf>)
  * [DC Volunteer Clients’ Right Advocacy](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/CC_VolunteerClientsRights_20190319.pdf>)

## Non-Profit Advocacy Groups

These advocacy groups are independent of the regional and developmental centers. They provide services to individuals with developmental disabilities.

  * [People First of California](<https://peoplefirstca.org/>)
  * [Disability Rights California](<http://www.disabilityrightsca.org/>)
