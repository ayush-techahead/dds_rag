# SEP/WAP Work Services Forms : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/work-services/service-providers-information/sep-wap-work-services-forms/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/work-services/service-providers-information/sep-wap-work-services-forms/)

---

These forms are to be used by work service  _(formerly Habilitation)_ providers and regional centers to process Work Services information. The Excel forms can be downloaded and completed using the Microsoft Excel spreadsheet program. The PDF instructions can be downloaded and printed using the free [Adobe Acrobat Reader](<https://get.adobe.com/reader/>). For further information, please read the instructions accompanying each form.

Supported Employment Program – Individual Forms

**DS 1961 SEP – SEP/Individual Habilitation Services Plan**  
This form is the standardized service plan Supported Employment Program (SEP). Providers use to implement the consumer’s vocational goals of the Individual Program Plan (IPP).

**Users:** SEP service providers.

  * [DS 1961 SEP (6/2016)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/DS1961SEP.xls>)
  * [Instructions (6/2016)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_Instructions62016_20190219.pdf>)

**DS 1972 SEP IP – SEP/Supported Employment Individual Placement Consumer Monthly Report**  
Outcome data for consumers provided Supported Employment Program- Individual Placement services is documented on this form.

**Users:** SEP Services Providers; Regional Center can preload consumer names and identification numbers.

  * [DS 1972 SEP IP (10/2019)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/DS1972_SEP_IP_1019.xlsm>)
  * [Instructions](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_Instructions112018_20190219.pdf>)

Supported Employment Program – Group Forms

**DS 1962 – Request For New SEP Groups**  
A Supported Employment Program-Group Placement (SEP-GP) approval request is submitted by service providers to DDS to obtain approval of new SEP Groups.

**Users:** SEP-GP service providers.

  * [DS 1962 (1/2005)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/DS1962-1.xls>)
  * [Instructions (8/2012)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_Instructions082012_20190219.pdf>)
  * [DS 1962/Supplemental Narrative Summary Guidance Template (4/2020)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/DS1962_Supplemental-COVID-19-Health-and-Safety-04-2020.xlsx>)

**DS 1963 – Request For Approval Of SEP Group Changes**  
This form is submitted by service providers to request changes in a specific approved supported employment program group.

**Users:** SEP-GP service providers.

  * [DS 1963 (6/2016)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/DS1963-1.xlsx>)
  * [Instructions (6/2016)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_Instructions062016_20190219.pdf>)

**DS 1964 – Supported Employment Group Billing and Attendance Form**  
This form must be completed as an excel file, password protected and submitted to regional centers, DOR and DDS monthly. After downloading the file, enter the vendor information at the top of the form and consumer and job coach information up to the “Funded By” column. Save a copy of this file as a “master”. Should any problem arise with the form, the “master” is available to start over.

  * [DS 1964 Large](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/DS_1964_Large.xlsm>) (1-900 lines – April 2022) – 64Bit compatible Excel
  * [DS 1964 Large](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/LargeDS1964-3957.xlsm>) (1-900 lines – Rate $39.57) – 64Bit compatible Excel
  * [Macro Instructions](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_MacroInstructions_20190219.pdf>)

Vocational Rehabilitation Forms

**Users:** Regional Center Service Coordinators.

**Users:** Regional Center Service Coordinators and Habilitation Services Providers.
