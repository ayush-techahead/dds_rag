Employment Forms

### Supported Employment – Individual Placement

  * DS 1961 SEP – SEP/Individual Habilitation Services Plan: This form is the standardized service plan Supported Employment Program (SEP). Providers use to implement the consumer’s vocational goals of the Individual Program Plan (IPP). Users: SEP service providers. 
    * [DS 1961 SEP (6/2016)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/DS1961SEP.xls>)
    * [Instructions (6/2016)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_Instructions62016_20190219.pdf>)
  * DS 1972 SEP IP – SEP/Supported Employment Individual Placement Consumer Monthly Report: Outcome data for consumers provided Supported Employment Program- Individual Placement services are documented on this form. Users: SEP Services Providers; Regional Center can preload consumer names and identification numbers. 
    * [DS 1972 SEP IP (10/2019)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/DS1972_SEP_IP_1019.xlsm>)
    * [Instructions](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_Instructions112018_20190219.pdf>)

### Supported Employment – Group Placement

  * DS 1962 – Request For New SEP Groups: A Supported Employment Program-Group Placement (SEP-GP) approval request is submitted by service providers to DDS to obtain approval of new SEP Groups. Users: SEP-GP service providers. 
    * [1962 (1/2005)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/DS1962-1.xls>)
    * [Instructions (8/2012)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_Instructions082012_20190219.pdf>)
    * [1962/Supplemental Narrative Summary Guidance Template (4/2020)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/DS1962_Supplemental-COVID-19-Health-and-Safety-04-2020.xlsx>)
  * DS 1963 – Request For Approval of SEP Group Changes: This form is submitted by service providers to request changes in a specific approved supported employment program group. Users: SEP-GP service providers. 
    * [1963 (6/2016)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/DS1963-1.xlsx>)
    * [Instructions (6/2016)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_Instructions062016_20190219.pdf>)
  * DS 1964 – Supported Employment Group Billing and Attendance Form: This form must be completed as an excel file, password protected and submitted to regional centers, DOR and DDS monthly. After downloading the file, enter the vendor information at the top of the form and consumer and job coach information up to the “Funded By” column. Save a copy of this file as a “master”. Should any problem arise with the form, the “master” is available to start over. 
    * [1964 Large (1–900 lines – April 2022) – 64Bit compatible](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/DS_1964_Large.xlsm>)
    * [Macro Instructions](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_MacroInstructions_20190219.pdf>)

### Work Activity Program

  * DS 1961 WAP – WAP/Individual Habilitation Services Plan: This form is the standardized service plan Work Activity Program (WAP). Providers use to implement the consumer’s vocational goals of the Individual Program Plan (IPP). Users: WAP service providers. 
    * [DS 1961 WAP (6/2016)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/DS1961WAP-1.xls>)
    * [Instructions (6/2016)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_WAPInstructions62016_20190219.pdf>)
  * DS 1971 WAP – WAP/Work Activity Program Consumer Monthly Report: Outcome data for consumers provided Work Activity Program services is documented on this form. Users: WAP Services Providers; Regional Center can preload consumer names and identification numbers. 
    * [DS 1971 WAP (7/2006)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/DS1971WAP-2.xls>)
    * [Instructions](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/WorkServices_Instructions072006_20190219.pdf>)

### Other

  * DS 1968 – Vocational Rehabilitation Referral: This form is used to refer consumers to the Department of Rehabilitation for Vocational Rehabilitation Services. Users: Regional Center Service Coordinators.
