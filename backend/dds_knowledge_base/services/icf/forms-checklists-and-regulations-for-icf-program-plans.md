## When Your Program Plan Is Completed, Submit the Following to the Email Listed Below:

  1. Program Plan packet and attachments
  2. Consultants qualifying documents
  3. Completed checklists

California Department of Developmental Services  
Program and Policy Section  
Email: [HealthFacilities@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:HealthFacilities@dds.ca.gov>)

The appropriate staff will review your plan for clarity and content and will contact you if changes to your Program Plan are required.

The Nurse Consultant will review and approve the Medication Training Plans, Attendant Training, and Specialized Procedures.

If you have any questions regarding this process, contact the Aging Services Branch by phone: 916-654-1965; or Email: [HealthFacilities@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:HealthFacilities@dds.ca.gov>).
