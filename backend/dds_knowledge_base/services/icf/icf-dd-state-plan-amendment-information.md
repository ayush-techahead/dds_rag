# ICF/DD State Plan Amendment Information : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/icf/icf-dd-state-plan-amendment-information/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/icf/icf-dd-state-plan-amendment-information/)

---

The ICF/DD State Plan Amendment (SPA) affecting ICF/DD, ICF/DD-H and ICF/DD-N (ICF) residential providers was approved April 15, 2011. This SPA is known as the ICF/DD SPA and makes changes retroactive to July 2007. The ICF/DD SPA provides for federal financial participation in the funding of specified day and related transportation services received by ICF residents, pursuant to their regional center individual program plan.
