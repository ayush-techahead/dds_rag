## What's New in Employment

**March 2026:**  
The Department released guidance on enrollment for the Coordinated Career Pathways (‘Pathways’) pilot and separate guidance on the newly established Supported Employment – Individual Placement Job Development subcode.  
[Coordinated Career Pathways Pilot](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/C-2026-Employment-001.pdf >)  
[Update: Guidelines for Job Development](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/G-2025-Employment-001REV.pdf>)

**September 2025:**  
The Department released guidance on the new Job Development subcode for Supported Employment – Individual Placement.  
[Guidelines for Job Development](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/G-2025-Employment-001_JobDevelopmentServiceDescription_and_ProgramAddendum.pdf>)  
[Job Development Service Code Description](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/G-2025-Employment-001_JobDevelopmentServiceDescription_and_ProgramAddendumAttachmentA.pdf>)

The Competitive Integrated Employment incentive payments have reverted to their original amount. Full details can be found in the directive below.  
[Guidelines for CIE Incentive Payments](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/C-2025-Employment-002_Guidelines_for_CompetitiveIntegratedEmploymentIncentivePayments.pdf>)
