Resources for Hiring and Retention 

  * [**Employer Assistance and Resource Network on Disability (EARN):**](<https://askearn.org/>) A free, nationwide service that provides education on strategies for recruiting, hiring, retaining, and advancing people with disabilities. 
  * [**EARN Success Stories:**](<https://askearn.org/employerprofiles>) Learn what employers are doing to ensure people with disabilities are part of workforce development.
  * [**Transformative Autism Program:**](<https://tapautism.org/>) Education on how to hire, train, and retain people with Autism.
  * [**Small Business Association:**](<https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business/hire-employees-disabilities>) Learn how to find talent and community partners.
  * [**Windmills:**](<https://www.miltwright.com/>) Disability inclusion training and resources.
  * [**Job Accommodation Network (JAN):**](<https://askjan.org/>) For free expert advice on accommodations.

Employer Tax Incentive

  * [**Tax Incentives for Employers:**](<https://www.dol.gov/agencies/odep/program-areas/employers/tax-incentives-for-employers>) Private-sector businesses my be eligible for tax incentives.
