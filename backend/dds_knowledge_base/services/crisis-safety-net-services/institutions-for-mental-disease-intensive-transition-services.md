# Intensive Transition Services for Individuals : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/crisis-safety-net-services/institutions-for-mental-disease-intensive-transition-services/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/crisis-safety-net-services/institutions-for-mental-disease-intensive-transition-services/)

---

The Department expanded vendor-operated services to include intensive transition services (also referred to as ITS). These services are offered to individuals with intellectual and/or developmental disabilities and co-occurring psychiatric disabilities. ITS provides additional support to individuals moving from an IMD, PDC, or restrictive setting to a less restrictive setting in the community.

## Characteristics of Services

Intensive transition services include the following:

  * Risk assessments;
  * Developing comprehensive Person-Centered Plans;
  * Making recommendations to planning teams;
  * Providing intensive preparatory services to individuals and their Interdisciplinary Teams;
  * Training and evaluation pre-transition and during transition as identified in the Individual Program Plan;
  * Supporting individuals with complex service needs during the transition process;
  * Training families and community-based service providers.
