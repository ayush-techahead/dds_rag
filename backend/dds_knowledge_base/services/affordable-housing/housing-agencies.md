# Housing Agencies : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/affordable-housing/housing-agencies/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/affordable-housing/housing-agencies/)

---

## [Access Home](<http://www.accesshome.org/>)

**Contact:** 530-224-8704

AccessHome (formerly Resources for Rural Community Development), based in Redding, California, assists individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities with home ownership and accessing affordable housing in their communities. AccessHome covers the nine-county area receiving services from Far Northern Regional Center.

## [Bay Area Housing Corporation (BAHC)](<https://www.bahc1.org/>)

**Contact:** 408-395-5100

BAHC, based in Los Gatos, California, creates quality housing opportunities for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities and other low income populations. BAHC works primarily in the San Francisco Bay area and enjoys a partnership with San Andreas Regional Center and Golden Gate Regional Center.

## [Brilliant Corners](<http://brilliantcorners.org/>)

**Contact:** 415-612-0012 _OR_[brilliantcorners@brilliantcorners.org](<mailto:brilliantcorners@brilliantcorners.org>)

Brilliant Corners (formerly West Bay Housing Corporation), based in San Francisco, California, acquires and renovates housing statewide for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

## [California Housing Access Services](<https://personcenteredhousing.com/>)

**Contact:** 805-267-9002

California Housing Access Services, based in Santa Barbara, California, provides person-centered Housing Access Services to individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities and assists entities, organizations, and vendors statewide to assess, develop, and implement person-centered housing programs and services.

## [California Housing Foundation (CHF)](<http://www.chfcares.com/>)

**Contact:** 909-793-9800

CHF, based in Redlands, California, supports the mission of the California Regional Centers by providing quality services and support programs designed to encourage and enable individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities achieve their highest possible level of personal independence, choice, and productivity.

## [Coalition for Housing Accessibility, Needs, Choices & Equality (CHANCE)](<https://chancehousing.org/>)

**Contact:** 805-966-0200

CHANCE, based in Santa Barbara, California, provides an array of housing services to assist individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities obtain affordable, accessible, community-based housing in California.

## [Housing Choices Coalition (HCC)](<http://www.housingchoices.org/>)

**Contact:** 408-498-5777

HCC, based in San Jose, California, serves the four-county area covered by the San Andreas Regional Center. HCC develops affordable housing units and provides vital housing services.

## [Housing Consortium of the East Bay (HCEB)](<http://hceb.org/>)

**Contact:** 510-832-1382

HCEB, based in Oakland, California, creates programs for home ownership, housing education, as well as develops affordable rental unit and housing education for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities. HCEB serves counties of the Regional Center of the East Bay.

## [Housing Now](<http://housingnowresource.org/>)

**Contact:** 916-549-1044

Housing Now, based in Sacramento, California, is a membership organization dedicated to expanding affordable and accessible housing options for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

## [Home Ownership Made Easy (HOME)](<https://www.homeopeningdoors.org/>)

**Contact:** 310-258-4131 _OR_[info@homeopeningdoors.org](<mailto:info@homeopeningdoors.org>)

HOME, based in Culver City, California, serves the greater Los Angeles metropolitan area. HOME creates and manages permanent, affordable, accessible, safe and sanitary rental housing for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities and facilitates independent community living options.

## [Home Ownership and Personal Empowerment (HOPE)](<http://www.hope-homes.org/>)

**Contact:** 310-543-0120 _OR_[info@hope-homes.org](<mailto:info@hope-homes.org>)

HOPE, based in Torrance, California, creates stable, affordable housing options for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities through three programs: affordable housing, permanent state-licensed group homes, and the College to Career program.

## [North Bay Housing Coalition (NBHC)](<https://www.northbayhousingcoalition.org/>)

**Contact:** 707-289-7000 _OR_[mary@northbayhousing.org](<mailto:mary@northbayhousing.org>)

NBHC, based in Napa, California, develops affordable housing units, home ownership, and housing education for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities who live in the three-county area served by North Bay Regional Center.

## [Scioto](<https://www.scioto.com/>)

**Contact:** 800-930-2892

Scioto, based in Powell, Ohio, finds and purchases properties that are safe, convenient and affordable homes for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities. Scioto works with provider organizations, state agencies, families, and individuals to ensure the homes are specific to the needs of the individuals who will live there.

## [Southern California Housing Collaborative (SCHC)](<http://www.socalhc.org/>)

**Contact:** 858-514-7009 _OR_ [nathan@socalhc.org](<mailto:nathan@socalhc.org>)

SCHC, based in San Diego, California, creates affordable housing opportunities for individuals with developmental disabilities who live in the counties served by San Diego Regional Center.

## [Tri-Counties Community Housing Corporation (TCCHC)](<https://www.clearinghousecdfi.com/impact_story/tcchc/>)

**Contact:** 805-456-8066 _OR_ [mbelfortti@tri-countieshousing.org](<mailto:mbelfortti@tri-countieshousing.org>)

TCCHC, based in Santa Barbara, California, secures and maintains safe, accessible, affordable and sustainable residential properties for individuals with developmental disabilities served by regional centers.

## [United Cerebral Palsy of Los Angeles, Ventura & Santa Barbara Counties (UCPLA)](<https://www.ucpla.org/>)

**Contact:** 818-782-2211

UCPLA, based in Woodland Hills, California, develops affordable housing for individuals with developmental disabilities in the greater Los Angeles, Ventura, and Santa Barbara areas.

## [Women Organizing Resources, Knowledge + Services (WORKS)](<http://www.worksusa.org/>)

**Contact:** 323-341-7028

WORKS, based in Los Angeles, California, delivers quality affordable housing and innovative enriched services. WORKS builds equitable, sustainable communities and encourages resident self-determination.
