# Canyon Springs : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/state-facilities/canyon-springs/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/state-facilities/canyon-springs/)

---

The mission of Canyon Springs Community Facility (CSCF) is to provide an individualized, person-centered approach in developing programs to maximize the potential of the individual and lead them to their preferred future. Individuals come to CSCF from regional center referrals, with a WIC 6500 commitment, as they are transitioning from Porterville Developmental Center (PDC). The CSCF team supports the individual by working with them to develop an Individual Program Plan (IPP) based on what is “important to” and “important for” the individual. 

While at Canyon Springs, individuals work on skills like coping skills, self-awareness, exercising rights and self-advocacy, safe and healthy choices, independence, and socialization. Individuals have opportunities to participate in a variety of integrated activities in natural environments like at home, work, and in the community. CSCF also provides individuals with career and technical education training.

## History

Opened in December 2000, CSCF is a 57,000 square foot community facility leased and operated by the State Department of Developmental Services as an alternative to larger Developmental Centers. CSCF is designed to provide residential services, treatment, and training for up to 55 adults who have developmental and intellectual disabilities. 

CSCF training and treatment is trauma-informed, person-centered, and focuses on the development of the individuals’ ability to manage their life through various treatment/training opportunities such as behavioral supports and replacement behavior teaching, coping skills, life skills, supportive counselling, vocational skills, adult education, recreational skills, interpersonal relationship development, community integration and encouragement of healthy life choices that lead to healthy, happy and productive lives.
