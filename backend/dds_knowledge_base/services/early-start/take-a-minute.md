# Take a Minute - Relationships Matter : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/early-start/take-a-minute/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/early-start/take-a-minute/)

---

![Take A Minute. Relationships Matter](https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/DDS_HomepageHero_ESTakeAMin-250x179.png)Take a Minute… Relationships Matter! Resource provides information to families and providers about the importance of social and emotional development. Individuals working in the Early Start community and parents are encouraged to download and print or view the resource below. Additional resources, including the Early Start Online Skill Base course on Facilitating Social and Emotional Development and the Take a Minute… Relationships Matter! Best Practices Checklist for Providers (2016) are available from the California [Early Start Neighborhood](<https://earlystartneighborhood.ning.com/ssip-resources/Take+a+Minute>) website.
