# Fiscal Year 2026-27 Part C Early Education Grant Guidelines for Applicants : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/early-start/fiscal-year-2026-27-part-c-early-education-grant-guidelines-for-applicants/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/services/early-start/fiscal-year-2026-27-part-c-early-education-grant-guidelines-for-applicants/)

---

For Fiscal Year 2026-27, the Department assumes responsibility to allocate the federal Part C Early Education funds to Special Education Local Plan Areas (SELPAs) and Local Educational Agencies (LEAs) that support infants and toddlers with solely low-incidence disabilities. For SELPAs and LEAs receiving the Part C, Early Education Program funds, refer to the following documents to complete and submit to the Department by the corresponding dates:

  * [Application Form ](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/ApplicationPartC_Form_FY_2026-27.pdf>)
    * Due May 29, 2026
  * [Budget Summary Form](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/BudgetSummaryPartC_Form_FY_2026-27.pdf>)
    * Due May 29, 2026
  * [Expenditure Form (Interim and Final)](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/ExpenditureClaimPartC_Form_FY_2026-27.pdf>)
    * Interim Due February 1, 2027
    * Final Due September 1, 2027
  * [Exhibit H – General Assurances and Certifications](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/EarlyEducationProgram_PartC_ExhibitH_FY_2026-27.pdf>)
    * Information only
  * [Guidance Document](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/PartC_Guidance_FY2026-27.pdf>)
    * Information only
