**Join a virtual town hall meeting.**

Service Providers are invited to a conversation about LOIS, a new system being built to replace outdated technology and better support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities, their families, regional centers, and the providers who serve them.

These sessions are not explaining the system, we really want to hear your ideas about how a system like LOIS could support you. For example, what would make it easier when you’re applying to be a vendor? What kinds of information would you want to share about your services? What information from regional centers about the people you serve would be most useful to you? What would make billing simpler? And beyond that, what other ways could a modern system help you give the best care to the people you serve?
