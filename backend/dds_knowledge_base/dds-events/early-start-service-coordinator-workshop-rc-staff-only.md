The Department is pleased to announce that registration is now open for the 2026 Early Start Service Coordinator Day workshops (one in Northern CA and one in Southern CA). These in-person workshops are designed to enhance collaboration, support professional development, and foster connections locally and state wide. 

**Who Should Attend:**

  * Early Start Service Coordinators and Managers from Regional Centers and Local Educational Agencies.
  * Early Start professionals serving infants and toddlers (& their families) with developmental delays or at risk of developing a disability.

Register early, these workshops will fill up! 

There is no cost to attend.

The same information will be shared on both dates. Please only register for one workshop. 

**Please note:** registration may be limited due to venue capacity. If capacity is reached, you will be able to sign up for a wait list in case there are cancellations.
