You are invited to join a webinar on Wednesday, April 29, 2026, to review the directive regarding updates to the Self-Determination Program (SDP) Orientation. Completion of the SDP Orientation is required before someone can participate in SDP.

The SDP Orientation has been updated with information designed to help individuals make an informed decision about whether to participate in the SDP. Effective April 1, 2026, the SDP Orientation consists of two parts, which are offered via Zoom by the [State Council on Developmental Disabilities](<https://scdd.ca.gov/sdp-orientation/>).

**Who Should Attend?**

  * Individuals considering participation in the SDP
  * Interested community members, service providers, and regional center staff

**Topics Covered:**

  * [Plain language](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/D-2026-SelfDeterminationProgram-002_PLS.pdf>) overview of [Directive D-2026-Self Determination Program-002](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/D-2026-Self-DeterminationProgram-002.pdf>) released March 24, 2026
