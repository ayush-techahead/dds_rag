## Event Materials

  * [ 2026-Career-Fair-Flyer-General ](<https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-Career-Fair-Flyer-General.pdf>)

The Department invites job seekers to a free career fair on April 7, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. in the lobby of the Allenby building (1215 O St, Sacramento, CA 95814).

The event is open to the public and will provide an opportunity to learn about the wide range of careers available within the Department with dedicated tables hosted by representatives from various program areas.

In addition to general application assistance, qualified candidates who complete the required exam and apply prior to the event will have the opportunity to participate in on-site interviews for Psychiatric Technicians, Psychiatric Technician Assistants (Certified Nursing Assistants), and Behavior Specialists.

If you would like an opportunity to interview or have any questions, please email [examhelpdesk@dds.ca.gov.](<mailto:examhelpdesk@dds.ca.gov> "mailto:examhelpdesk@dds.ca.gov")
