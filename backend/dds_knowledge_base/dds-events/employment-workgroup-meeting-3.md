The next Employment Workgroup meeting will be on Wednesday, Oct. 8, from 10:00 a.m. to noon.

The Employment Workgroup started in 2021 to bring in community voices and feedback on employment topics. Since then, the group has helped the Department by sharing input on many employment-related issues.

## At the October meeting:

  * The Department will share information on concepts to update employment services
  * Workgroup members will give comments and suggestions on different parts of the presentation
  * The public can also attend, listen in, and provide feedback
