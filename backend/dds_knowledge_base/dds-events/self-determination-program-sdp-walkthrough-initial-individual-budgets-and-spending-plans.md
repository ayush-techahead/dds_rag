Dear Self-Determination Program Community,

You are invited to join a one hour webinar on Friday December 12, 2025. This webinar will provide an overview of the most recent SDP directive “D-2025-Self-Determination Program-003: Initial Individual Budgets and Spending Plans.”

D-2025-Self-Determination Program-003 provides regional centers direction on implementing changes to the SDP that are required by Assembly Bill (AB)143. These changes include:

  * How the initial individual budget is calculated
  * Certification of spending plans
  * Transfer of spending plan funds
