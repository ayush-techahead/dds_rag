**Join an in-person town hall meeting.**

These in-person listening sessions are only for local individuals and families who receive regional center services.

Space is limited to 50 participants at each event to allow for productive conversations. Individuals and families can also share their input by email, the online LOIS form and 2 upcoming webinars, you don’t have to attend in-person to share what you want to see in the LOIS system.
