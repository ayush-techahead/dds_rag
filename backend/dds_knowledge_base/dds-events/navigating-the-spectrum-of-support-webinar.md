[Join](<https://hellobrightline.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_SlS06Vu0SEaLHiI9RWLRUA#/registration>) **BrightLife Kids** and the **Department** on **April 30, 2026** , 12:00-1:00pm PST for an informative webinar: **“**[**Navigating the Spectrum of Support**](<https://hellobrightline.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_SlS06Vu0SEaLHiI9RWLRUA#/registration>)**.”**

BrightLife Kids partners with all 21 regional centers across the state of California to connect families with vital behavioral health and specialized services. This session is designed for parents, community partners, and regional center staff to explore how our organizations collaborate to create a seamless web of support.

**In this webinar, we will cover:**

  * Comprehensive service outlines for both BrightLife Kids and regional centers.
  * Insights into our collaborative referral and support processes.
  * How we can work together to best serve our local communities.

**Live Spanish translation support will be available.**
