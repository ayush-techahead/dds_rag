_**Register today to learn about your rights!** _The HCBS and Your Rights in-person workshop is designed to help you better understand your rights.

It’s a hands-on, interactive training hosted in partnership with Alo Consultation and the Department of Developmental Services.

You will learn about your right to choice, independence and full participation in everyday life and the community.

Please Note: **This is a training for people 18 and older with intellectual and/or****developmental disabilities, not the staff who support them.** Staff are welcome if needed to support you to attend or participate in the workshop.

**What are HCBS Rights?**  
Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) rights are the rights of people who receive services from regional centers and have an intellectual and/or developmental disability. These rights have to do with choice, community, employment, relationships, visitors, food, and more!
