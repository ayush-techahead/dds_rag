This meeting will focus on improving coordination in employment efforts and making it easier for individuals to access services. 

We appreciate your participation and look forward to a productive discussion as we work together to improve employment outcomes for the individuals we serve.

At this meeting, participants will:

  * Learn how departments are coordinating service delivery for employment opportunities. 
  * Discuss Regional Center employment services and the recently announced changes at Department of Rehabilitation. 
  * Hear comments and suggestions from workgroup panelists on priorities for the employment team over the next year. 

This meeting is open to the public.
