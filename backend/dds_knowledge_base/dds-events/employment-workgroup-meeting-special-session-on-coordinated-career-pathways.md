**Topics Covered**

  * Learn about the Pathways Pilot, including funding and current spending
  * Overview of pilot utilization statewide
  * Review one-time funding for the pilot and discuss next steps
  * Hear comments and suggestions from workgroup panelists on Coordinated Career Pathways

**Who Should Attend**  
Workgroup members, members of the public and anyone interested in learning more about Coordinated Career Pathways.
