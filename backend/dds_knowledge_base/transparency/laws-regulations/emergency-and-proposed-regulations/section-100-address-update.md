# Section 100 - Address Update : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/section-100-address-update/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/section-100-address-update/)

---

## Description

The Department of Developmental Services has amended section 100 changes without regulatory effect. The changes reflect the Department’’’s new address. These changes meet the requirements of CCR, title 1, section 100 because the revisions are a “change without regulatory effect.”

## Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17. Public Health  
Division 2. Health and Welfare Agency-Department of Developmental Services  
Subchapter 3. Department of Developmental Services – Conflict of Interest Code and  
Subchapter 5. Procedural Safeguards  
Article 3. Complaint Process  
Amend section 52170
