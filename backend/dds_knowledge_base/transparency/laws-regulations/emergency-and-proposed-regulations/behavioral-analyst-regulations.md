## (Proposed Regulations)

### Description

The Department of Developmental Services (DDS) proposes to amend Title 17, California Code of Regulations, Division 2, Chapter 3, Subchapter 2 by amending sections 54342 and 57332 to add the category of Associate Behavior Analyst to the list of vendors.

### Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17 California Code of Regulations, Sections 54342 and 57332.
