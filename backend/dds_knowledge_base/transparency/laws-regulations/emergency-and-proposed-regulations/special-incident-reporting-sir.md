# Special Incident Reporting (SIR) : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/special-incident-reporting-sir/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/special-incident-reporting-sir/)

---

The Department of Developmental Services has updated sections in California Code of Regulations, title 17, regulations for special incident reporting to incorporate definitions and provide clarifications necessary for consistency of special incident reporting across all regional centers.

## Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17. Public Health  
Division 2. Health and Welfare Agency—Department of Developmental Services Regulations  
Chapter 3. Community Services  
Subchapter 2. Vendorization  
Amend sections 54327, 54327.1, 56002, 56026, 56038, 56059 and 56093
