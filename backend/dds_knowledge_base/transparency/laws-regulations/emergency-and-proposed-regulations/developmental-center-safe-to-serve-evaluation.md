# Developmental Center Safe-to-Serve Evaluation : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/developmental-center-safe-to-serve-evaluation/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/developmental-center-safe-to-serve-evaluation/)

---

The Department of Developmental Services has adopted sections in title 17, California Code of Regulations, pertaining to the Developmental Center Safe-to-Serve Process, which addresses the procedures for determining whether a court-committed individual will be denied admission to a DDS Developmental Center under Welfare and Institutions Code section 6510.5.

## Affected Regulatory Sections

Title 17. Public Health  
Division 2. Health and Welfare Agency – Department of Developmental Services  
Chapter 1. General Provisions  
Subchapter 11. Developmental Center Safe to Serve Evaluation Process  
Article 1. General  
Adopted sections 51100, 51101, 51102, 51103, 51104, 51105, and 51106.
