# Participant Directed Services : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/participant-directed-services/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/participant-directed-services/)

---

## Description

The Department proposes amendments to California Code of Regulations (CCR) title 17, to add additional services to the list of participant directed services, in order for consumers to choose someone they are familiar with to provide services which increases the chances for consumers to obtain services.

## Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17. Public Health  
Division 2. Health and Welfare Agency – Department of Developmental Services  
Chapter 3. Community Services  
Amend sections 57310, 57332, 58886 and 58888
