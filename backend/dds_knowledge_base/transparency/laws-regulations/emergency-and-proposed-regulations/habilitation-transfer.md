# Habilitation Transfer : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/habilitation-transfer/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/habilitation-transfer/)

---

**(Emergency Regulations)**

**Description**

The Department of Developmental Services (DDS) proposes to adopt and amend regulations to: Transfer the Administrative responsibility for the Habilitation Service Program (HSP) from the Department of Rehabilitation (DOR) to the DDS effective 7/1/04 pursuant to AB 1753.

**Affected Regulatory Code Sections**

Title 17, California Code of Regulations, Sections 54302, 54310, 54320, 54351, 54370, 58800, 58801, 58810, 58811, 58812, 58820, 58821, 58822, 58830, 58831, 58832, 58833, 58834, 58840, 58841, 58842, 58850, 58851, 58860, 58861, 58862, 58863, 58864, 58870, 58871, 58872, 58873, 58874, 58875, 58876, 58877, 58878, 58879, 58880, 58881 and 58882.
