# Early Intervention Services – Part C : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/early-intervention-services-part-c/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/early-intervention-services-part-c/)

---

The Department proposes amendments to California Code of Regulations (CCR) title 17, for early intervention services to align the State’s regulations with Part C of IDEA, which is required for the State to obtain federal funding for early intervention services for eligible infants, toddlers, and their families who are in the early start program.

## Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17. Public Health  
Division 2. Health and Welfare Agency  
Chapter 2. Early Intervention Services  
Amend sections 52000, 52082, 52109, and 52162
