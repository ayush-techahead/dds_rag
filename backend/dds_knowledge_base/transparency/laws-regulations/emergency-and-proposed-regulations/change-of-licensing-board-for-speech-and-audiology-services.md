# Section 100-Change of Licensing Board for Speech and Audiology Services : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/change-of-licensing-board-for-speech-and-audiology-services/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/change-of-licensing-board-for-speech-and-audiology-services/)

---

## Description

The Department of Developmental Services has amended section 54342 in title 17 of the California Code of Regulations, to reflect the correct name of the merged boards under the Department of Consumer Affairs (DCA); Hearing Aid Dispensers Bureau and the Speech-Language Pathology and Audiology Board to the Speech-Language Pathology and Audiology and Hearing Aid Dispensers Board.

## Description Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17. Public Health  
Division 2. Health and Welfare Agency—Department of Developmental Services Regulations  
Chapter 3. Community Services  
SubChapter 2. Vendorization  
Article 3. Vendor Numbers and Service Code  
Amend section 54342
