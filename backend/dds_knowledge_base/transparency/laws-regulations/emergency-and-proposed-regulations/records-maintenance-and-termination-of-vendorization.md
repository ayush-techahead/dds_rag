# Records Maintenance and Termination of Vendorization : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/records-maintenance-and-termination-of-vendorization/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/records-maintenance-and-termination-of-vendorization/)

---

## (Emergency/Proposed Regulations)

### Description

The Department of Developmental Services (Department) proposes to amend regulations to: (1) increase funding the State received from the federal government for consumers on the Home and Community-Based Waiver; and (2) give regional centers the authority to terminate vendorization which threatens the health and safety of the consumer(s).

### Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17, California Code of Regulations, Sections 50604, 50608, 54326, 54370, 56003, 56082, 57540, 58510, 58671.
