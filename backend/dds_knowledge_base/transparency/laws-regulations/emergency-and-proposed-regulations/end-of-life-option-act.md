# End of Life Option Action : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/end-of-life-option-act/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/end-of-life-option-act/)

---

## Description

The Department of Developmental Services has adopted sections in California Code of Regulations, title 17, regulations pertaining to the end of life option act. The rulemaking action creates a uniform policy to address the impact caused by the end of life option act.

## Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17. Public Health  
Division 2. Health and Welfare Agency-Department of Developmental Services Regulations  
Chapter 1. General Provisions  
Subchapter 10. End of Life Option Act  
Article 1. General  
Adopt sections: 51000, 51001, 51002
