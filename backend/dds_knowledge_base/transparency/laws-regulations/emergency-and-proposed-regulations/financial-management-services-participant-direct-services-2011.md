## Financial Management Services/Participant-Direct Services 2011

## (Emergency and Regular Regulations)

## **Description**

The Department of Developmental Services proposes to amend Title 17, California Code of Regulations, Division 2, Subchapters 2, 6, 18, and 22, by amending sections 50604, 54355, 58543, and adding new sections 58883, 58884, 58886, 58887 and 58888, to add the use of a financial management services provider for voucher services to be known as Participant-Directed Services.

## **Affected Regulatory Code Sections**

Title 17, California Code of Regulations, sections 50604, 54355, 58543, 58883, 58884, 58886, 58887 and 58888.

The regulatory actions for the regular rulemaking are completed.
