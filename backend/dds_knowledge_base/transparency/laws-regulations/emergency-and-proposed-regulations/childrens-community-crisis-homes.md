# Children’s Community Crisis Homes : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/childrens-community-crisis-homes/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/childrens-community-crisis-homes/)

---

## Withdrawn 9/14/20

### Description

The Department proposes to amend California Code of Regulations, Title 17, sections 59000, 59002, 59006, 59007, 59008, 59009, 59010, 59012, 59022, 59072, and the incorporated documents on an emergency basis to address an emergency and is necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health and safety, or general welfare for purposes of Sections 11346.1 and 11349.6 of the Government Code. The proposed regulations will enable consumers eligible to reside in Children’s Community Crisis Homes to avoid placement in more restrictive settings.

### Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17. Public Health  
Division 2. Health and Welfare Agency – Department of Developmental Services Regulations  
Chapter 3. Community Services  
Subchapters 23 and 24 Enhanced Behavioral Supports Homes.  
Amend Sections 59000, 59002, 59006, 59007, 59008, 59009, 59010, 59012, 59022, 59072, and the incorporated documents.
