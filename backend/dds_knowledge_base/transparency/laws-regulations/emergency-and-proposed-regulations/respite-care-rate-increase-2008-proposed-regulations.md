# Respite Care Rate Increase 2008 (Proposed Regulations) : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/respite-care-rate-increase-2008-proposed-regulations/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/respite-care-rate-increase-2008-proposed-regulations/)

---

## Description

The Department of Developmental Services proposes to amend Title 17, California Code of Regulations, Division 2, Chapter 3, Subchapter 2, by amending sections 57310 and 57332, as a result of the minimum wage increase effective January 1, 2008.

### Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17, California Code of Regulations, section 57310 and 57332.

## Status

Notice of Proposed Rulemaking: February 26, 2008  
Last Day of 45-Day Comment Period: April 21, 2008  
Final text of the regulations filed with the Secretary of State: July 14, 2008  
Effective Date of Regulations: August 13, 2008
