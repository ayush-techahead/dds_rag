# Early Start Procedural Safeguards (Proposed Regulations) : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/early-start-procedural-safeguards-proposed-regulations/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/early-start-procedural-safeguards-proposed-regulations/)

## **Description**

The Department of Developmental Services (DDS) proposes to amend Title 17, California Code of Regulations (CCR), Division 2, Chapter 2, Sub Chapter 5: Procedural Safeguards.

## **Affected Regulatory Code Sections**

Title 17, California Code of Regulations, sections 52170, 52172 and 52173.

## **Status**

Notice of Proposed Rulemaking: April 30, 2010

Last Day of 45-Day Comment Period: June 14, 2010

Public Hearings: June 17, 2010 & July 15, 2010

Effective Date of Regulations: March 3, 2011
