# Enhanced Behavioral Supports Homes : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/enhanced-behavioral-supports-homes/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/enhanced-behavioral-supports-homes/)

---

## Description

The Department of Developmental Services has adopted sections in California Code of Regulations title 17, to enable eligible consumers to reside in enhanced behavioral supports homes and avoid placement in more restrictive or out-of-state living arrangements. This rulemaking implements SB 856 and the Lanterman Act, Welfare and Institutions Code section 4500 et seq., enacted to reduce institutionalization of people with developmental disabilities and prevent dislocation from home communities.
