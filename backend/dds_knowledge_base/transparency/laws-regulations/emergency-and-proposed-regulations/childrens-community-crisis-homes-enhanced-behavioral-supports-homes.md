# Children’s Community Crisis Homes/Enhanced Behavioral Supports Homes : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/childrens-community-crisis-homes-enhanced-behavioral-supports-homes/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/childrens-community-crisis-homes-enhanced-behavioral-supports-homes/)

## Description

The Department received approval for the Emergency Children’s Community Crisis Homes Regulations and is now undertaking the regular rulemaking process to make the regulations permanent with its Certificate of Compliance. Along with the endeavor to file the Certificate of Compliance for the Children’s Community Crisis Homes regulations, the Department wanted to provide consistency by aligning the regulations for Enhanced Behavioral Supports Homes (EBSH) and including them with this rulemaking.

## Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17. Public Health  
Division 2. Health and Welfare Agency  
Chapter 3. Community Services  
Subchapter 23. Community Crisis Homes and  
Subchapter 24. Enhanced Behavioral Supports Homes

Adopt new articles 5.5 and 6.5 and new sections 59009.5, 59010.1, 59010.2, 59010.3, 59010.4, and 59010.5 subchapter 23, title 17, CCR, for CCH.

Adopt new articles 5.5 and 6.5 and new sections 59060.1, 59060.2, 59060.3, 59060.4, and 59060.5, subchapter 24, title 17, CCR, for EBSH.
