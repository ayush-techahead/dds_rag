# Behavior Management Consultant : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/behavior-management-consultant/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/behavior-management-consultant/)

---

## Description

The Department of Developmental Services has amended the California Code of Regulations, title 17, by extending the deadline by which behavior management consultants must acquire 12 semester units in applied behavior analysis. 

## Affected Regulatory Code Sections

Title 17. Public Health  
Division 2. Health and Welfare Agency – Department of Developmental Services  
Chapter 3. Community Services  
Subchapter 2. Vendorization  
Article 3. Vendor Numbers and Service Codes  
Amend section 54342
