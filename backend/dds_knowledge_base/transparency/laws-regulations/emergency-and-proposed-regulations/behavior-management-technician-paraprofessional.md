# Behavior Management Technician (Paraprofessional) : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/behavior-management-technician-paraprofessional/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/emergency-and-proposed-regulations/behavior-management-technician-paraprofessional/)

---

## Description

The Department of Developmental Services has amended sections in California Code of Regulations, title 17, to add Behavior Management Technician (Paraprofessional) – Service Code 616. This regulatory action addresses the use of paraprofessionals in group practice provider behavioral intervention services and establishes a service code 616 with a rate, educational/experiential qualifications and professional supervision requirements for this newly designated service provider. This rulemaking is to implement part of AB 104 (Statut~s of 2011, Chapter 37).
