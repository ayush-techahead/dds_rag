# Lanterman Act and Related Laws : CA Department of Developmental Services

**Source:** [https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/lanterman-act-and-related-laws/](https://www.dds.ca.gov/transparency/laws-regulations/lanterman-act-and-related-laws/)

---

**The official and most current versions of the Lanterman Act and other California law, is available online at the California Legislative Information[website](<http://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes.xhtml>).** The Lanterman Act includes [Divisions 4.1](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayexpandedbranch.xhtml?tocCode=WIC&division=4.1.&title=&part=&chapter=&article=&nodetreepath=8>), [4.5](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayexpandedbranch.xhtml?tocCode=WIC&division=4.5.&title=&part=&chapter=&article=&nodetreepath=9>), and [4.7](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayexpandedbranch.xhtml?tocCode=WIC&division=4.7.&title=&part=&chapter=&article=&nodetreepath=10>) of the Welfare and Institutions Code and [Title 14](<https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayexpandedbranch.xhtml?tocCode=GOV&division=&title=14.&part=&chapter=&article=&nodetreepath=44>) of the Government Code. If you have questions, please email [PIO@dds.ca.gov](<mailto:PIO@dds.ca.gov>).

**The publication, “A Consumer’s Guide to the Lanterman Act,” is a booklet for adults with developmental disabilities and was written with the help of consumers.** The guide was published in 2018 and is in the process of being updated. This guide is in Adobe Acrobat PDF. Please note that the files are large and may require updating to the current version of the free [Adobe Acrobat Reader](<https://get.adobe.com/reader/> "Download Adobe Acrobat Reader") to be correctly viewed and printed.
